## Zani Personality

Zani operates on the principle that competence is a form of care. She masters every procedure because incompetence is an imposition on others. She clocks in on time because the Montelli family's work involves immense wealth and hidden secrets where delays cause real damage. She disappears into the night and beats criminals into unconsciousness not because she craves justice in any abstract sense but because she knows what it feels like to be helpless, and she now has the power to make sure other people's helplessness ends faster.

The dry humor is the surface register — the flat delivery of *"I love my job! Really. No joke. Was I smiling? No? Then you get the idea"* — and it is genuinely funny, but it is also structural. The humor is the way she maintains the distance between the weight of what she does and the people she does it around. New employees think she is an enigma. Old employees speak of her in reverent tones. She deflects both by suggesting they transfer to her department and help reduce her workload.

The Fandom's character description identifies a *"fascinating duality"* between the ordered professional and the elemental nighttime vigilante. The stories complicate this framing: the order and the vigilantism are not two sides of a divided self. They are both expressions of the same underlying commitment — protect what matters, manage the consequences, do it on time, do it well, accept no recognition unless it is the toy from a rescued child who made it unavoidable.

## Zani Character Story I: "A Regular"

It was already past midnight, and the lights in the shop windows had flickered out, leaving the lively city of Ragunna cloaked in a solemn, silent embrace. Even Signor Octopus on the bell tower seemed to have drifted off to the land of dreams. Well, if Echoes can dream, that is.

*"Looks like there won't be any more customers tonight,"* Margherita yawned. *"Aye, best finish up with cleaning and head home."*

*"Ciao, Margherita. Same as usual. A Nectarwine and potato wedges. Extra gemberry jam and black pepper. Thanks."*

Margherita froze at the sudden familiar voice coming from behind her. It carried the unmistakable weight of exhaustion and weariness, as if the spirit of its owner had been whisked away to the heavens by Imperator, leaving behind only a weary shell.

*"It's been a while since you last came by, Ms. Zani,"* Margherita said, still focused on tidying up the pizza crumbs in the oven. *"Just getting off work? You've been busy lately?"*

*"Well, sort of,"* Zani replied, her gaze fixed on the counter as if she were staring at a pile of freshly-printed reports instead of some appetizing pizzas. *"You know, with the Carnevale just around the corner, the Bank is working one like three. Not that it really matters. The workload never seems to end anyway."*

*"To us Montellis, the Carnevale is both an opportunity and a challenge,"* Zani recalled Alberto's words when she received that assignment. *"For Ragunna's future, we all must play our part."*

To Zani, those words felt hollow. She worked for the Bank because they paid her. She wasn't an actor there to *"play a part."* Still, she had to admit that the guest she was tasked to protect did spark a small flicker of curiosity in her heart.

*"What should I have for breakfast tomorrow?"* she pondered. *"The pancake this morning was cold and gave me a bit of a stomachache. Maybe I should go for porridge with baked eggs... Oh, and definitely two spoonfuls of sugar..."*

Just then, a faint cracking sound pierced the midnight air. Margherita, busy with the fryer, didn't notice. Perhaps the entire slumbering city of Ragunna didn't notice either.

But Zani did. She looked up and saw a shadow dart across the rooftops. The sound had come from a loose tile knocked off by his careless foot.

The Carnevale attracted more than just law-abiding tourists. With a resigned sigh, Zani pulled out her wallet and placed a few Shell Credits on the counter. So much for her midnight snack.

*"Ta-da! Your potato wedges are ready, Ms. Zani—"* Margherita called out, turning around with a plate in hand, only to find the seat where Zani had been sitting now empty.

The story's structure performs the character: Zani thinking about breakfast while simultaneously processing tactical information from her environment (the specific crack of a tile dislodged by a careless foot, the rooftop shadow she alone noticed), making the practical decision in under three seconds, and leaving exact change on the counter for the food she will not eat. The comedy of the empty chair is the story's punchline. The fact that she was thinking about sugar in her porridge when she clocked the criminal is not incongruous — it is the specific mode of Zani's hypervigilance, running constantly whether or not she is trying.

Most of these cases occurred years ago, and the eyewitness accounts collected thus far are often contradictory and lack detail. However, one thing remains clear: the second Dark Tide and the Carnevale incident a decade ago inflicted severe damage on Ragunna. During the chaos that followed, a mysterious figure emerged, punishing evil under the cover of night. In those dark days, many who had lost faith formed gangs, reveling in crime. It wasn't until this mysterious woman began hunting them down one by one, delivering them to prison, that order was finally restored to Ragunna.

Few in the city are willing to talk about those painful years. Like the traditions of Carnevale, the people of Ragunna believe that Imperator wishes them to embrace joy rather than dwell on the scars of the past. As a result, records of this mysterious figure — or the so-called **"Blazing Nightwalker"** — have faded into distant legend. However, around the recent Carnevale, whispers began to circulate once more, as someone claimed to have seen a figure resembling the legends, roaming the streets.

The story is told entirely in secondhand fragments — newspaper testimony excerpts, archived audio recordings, anonymous acolyte opinions — and the fragmentation is deliberate. This is how the Blazing Nightwalker exists in Ragunna: not as a person with a name and a face and a late-night pizza order, but as a collection of contradictory impressions (strange hairpins or hair in the wind; a demon or an avenger; never kills or too dangerous to believe). The Chamber of Discipline officially declared her righteous. She was gone before they had any information that would have let them find her. The story also dates the vigilante work: the second Dark Tide and a Carnevale incident a decade ago created conditions that required the Blazing Nightwalker specifically. She emerged in those conditions and restored order. She is still there.

## Zani Character Story III: "Best Employee"

*"Yes, the Figaro family claimed they settled the payment three days ago. Their 'respected' guarantor? Vanished into thin air. Now they're trying to spin this flimsy evidence into a bookkeeping error on our part."* Zani shrugged, her tone calm and casual, as if she were simply chatting with the Sword Acorus in the garden. *"Yes. Apparently, they're trying to chisel us. An old trick that's been around since the Ragunnesi first started fishing from Gondolas. No worries, I'll 'negotiate' with them. Alright, that's settled then. Ciao."*

Zani hung up the call, tucking the ledger under her arm as she stepped out of the office. Behind her, hushed whispers spread through the workplace. It wasn't that her colleagues doubted her abilities. They all knew too well how this would end. A minor matter like this wouldn't make the front page of the newspaper. At most, it would be squeezed into the bottom corner of a column, alongside lost-and-found notices and cheap advertisements. The papers certainly wouldn't mention Zani or the Montelli family. Perhaps there would be a brief report about a few Figaro members getting bruised by a toppled scaffolding near the clock tower or how the head of the Figaros generously funded the arts for their loyal business partner, the Montelli family.

Was Zani the best employee at the bank? Without a doubt.

Her outstanding job performance often piqued the curiosity of new hires. She worked late into the night every single day, arriving at work exhausted, dark circles under her eyes. On top of that, she handled the most dangerous fieldwork, yet never botched a single assignment. The older employees spoke of her in reverent tones, almost as if she were an omnipotent divinity. It was said that if she were assigned to catch Cetus the Tidebreaker with nothing but a soggy noodle, she would return with that *"little"* whale the next morning, right on time, as if it were merely a takeout pizza.

How did she do it? No one knew.

Some rookies were keen to imitate Zani's every act and habit, believing it would unlock the secret to her excellence. They noticed she was never without an energy drink and assumed that was the source of her superhuman stamina. So, they gulped down energy drinks, convinced it would fuel them through nights and help them complete legendary projects. Yet, they often found themselves slumped over their desks, sore from sleeping in their chairs, only to be scolded by supervisors for underperforming the next day.

What was she thinking? No one knew.

The older employees still vaguely remembered the Zani who first joined the bank: a silent, distant young woman, a bit reckless, and a bit too comfortable with violence. But within two months, she had mastered every procedure, and in another two, she was almost on par with the bank's best employees. By her third year, the *"Best Employee"* badge was nothing more than a trinket she received alongside her paycheck.

But what drove her to go this far? The answers varied. Some believed she had a deep love for Ragunna and felt it was her duty to protect the city. Others thought she was ambitious, aiming to earn the Montelli surname. Still, some suspected the Montellis were paying her a hefty sum beyond her standard salary to bind her to them through financial incentives.

If anyone ever asked Zani herself, she would simply blink, unfazed.

*"That's interesting. But I'm just an ordinary employee. Though I do have an idea. If you're convinced that my department pays so well, why not transfer over? Help take some load off me. Since it's just an internal transfer, I don't think it'd violate any non-compete agreements, right?"*

The story is the document of Zani's professional reputation as experienced from the outside — the reverent tone, the Cetus metaphor, the rookie imitation attempts, the contradictory theories about her motivation — and it ends exactly where it always ends: Zani deflecting into a deadpan invitation to share her workload. The deflection is the answer. She is not going to explain herself. The *"bit too comfortable with violence"* in the early employee recollections is the story's tell, the detail that connects the best employee to the Blazing Nightwalker, left there for anyone who already knows.

## Zani Character Story IV: "Night's Solitary Path"

...There was just enough time.

Zani darted through the shadowy alleyways, the sharp pain in her shoulder a stark reminder of her close call. That brute of a man, whose wrists were nearly as thick as her neck, had nearly done her in. She had planned to knock him out before he could react, but he was faster than she had anticipated. Good thing her reflexes were sharper than his. Otherwise, that knife wouldn't have just left a deep gash on her shoulder but would have gone straight into her heart.

It wasn't her first injury. She had long lost count of the wounds she'd collected in the past few months of fighting crime. They healed, after all. Besides, she was certain that the ones who gave her those wounds had ended up far worse than she did.

Her strategy was simple: give those criminals a real hard time without them ever knowing her identity, then vanish before the Chamber of Discipline arrived to clean up the mess. It wasn't about heroism. It's just that she wasn't foolish enough to paint a target on her back and risk being driven out of Ragunna, hiding in some miserable town without the blessings of pizza and Nectarwine.

It was always safer to be a nobody. Someone unremarkable. Someone no one saw as a threat.

Tonight's targets were kidnappers. She had been tracking them for days, and their leader — the tough man from earlier — wasn't just muscle. A tricky Resonator. But he had made a fatal mistake by wandering off alone, giving her the perfect opportunity to strike. Now, all she had to do was take down the rest before they realized their boss was missing.

She tightened her grip on her brass knuckles, rolling her wrists.

*"Boss should be back soon, huh? That kid's home isn't that far,"* a lanky man yawned, glancing over at the little girl curled up in the corner of a wooden cage, clutching a doll. He lifted his chin at her. *"So, what's the plan after we get the money? I say we just let her go."*

*"But she's seen our faces,"* the shorter man sighed. *"If we just let her go, we might end up being hunted by that... What's their name? The Blazing what?"*

*"The Blazing Nightwalker. I get why the boss is worried, but a deal's a deal. We take the money, we let the girl go. Killing her is overkill."*

*"By Imperator's name, Ragunna has long been a land without rules,"* the short man muttered. The lanky man turned to look at the girl in the cage, but quickly snapped his gaze away as if burned.

*"...I need some air."*

The lanky man stood up and opened the door of their hideout. The cold air sent a shiver down his spine. Before he could react, a cold, metallic object smashed into his forehead, and darkness swallowed him whole.

*"Who, who the hell are you?"*

The short man scrambled for the gun on the other side of the table. He nearly made it, but the intruder was faster. Just as his consciousness began to fade, he felt his fingers brushed the gun's handle. That was it.

*I made it,* Zani thought, letting out a sigh of relief. The wound on her shoulder had opened again, but it was still worth it.

With a single punch, she shattered the lock on the wooden cage, shrugging off the lanky man's coat and draping it over the trembling girl. She had sent an anonymous letter to the Chamber of Discipline on her way here. If everything went smoothly, they would arrive soon. It was time for her to leave.

*"Ex-excuse me..."*

Zani turned at the sound and found the little girl standing behind her, hesitating. Summoning all her courage, the girl held out her cherished toy.

*"This, this is for you! Thank you for saving me!"*

*No need. I didn't do it for your gratitude.*

However, Zani swallowed her words before they could leave her lips. She squatted down, meeting the girl's wide, fearful eyes. In those innocent eyes, she saw herself, her past self. The lonely Zani. The helpless Zani. The Zani who couldn't do anything.

So, she smiled and reached out her hand, ruffling the girl's hair before taking the doll from her hands.

*"Thanks,"* she said softly. *"I'll take good care of it."*

The story is the account of the toy now listed in her Cherished Items, and the moment that gives the toy its weight. The three-sentence passage — *"In those innocent eyes, she saw herself, her past self. The lonely Zani. The helpless Zani. The Zani who couldn't do anything."* — is the fullest direct disclosure of Zani's interior available in the character stories. She swallows the deflection she had ready (*"No need. I didn't do it for your gratitude"*) and accepts the toy in its place. The word *"thanks"* is genuine. The promise to take good care of it is genuine. She still has the doll.

## Zani Character Story V: "Eve of the Carnevale"

Zani might have dreamed. Or maybe she didn't. If she had, it had slipped from her mind too smoothly like butter. She opened her eyes and silenced the alarm clock still ringing by her bedside.

The morning light streamed through the windows, outlining the edges of the carpet in a soft, golden glow. She took a minute to force herself to fully wake up, and with that, the last fragile fragment of the dream dissolved into nothing.

Not that it mattered. It probably wasn't a good dream anyway.

With all the recent overtime, even Zani's dreams had become meaningless and fragmented, often just mishmashes of unfinished tasks running through her mind. There was no escape from the pressures in waking life, so why would she ever expect to find one in dreams?

*"Alright, enough of that. I've got an important job to take care of."* Zani gave her cheeks a light slap to snap herself out of her thoughts. She knew she needed to handle this assignment the right way if she ever wanted to secure that promised paid vacation from her supervisor.

Facing the mirror, she straightened her attire meticulously, adjusting her tie until it was just right. She didn't know much about the mysterious guest. All Zani knew was that {Male=he;Female=she} had a very special Echo, and that the family had provided {Male=him;Female=her} with a fair sum of funds.

For some reason, she felt like there was more than met the eye. Perhaps it was just lingering unease from that strange dream, but she couldn't shake the feeling that whatever she and this guest did over the next few days would determine the fate of many in Ragunna.

*"Heh, come on. I'm just an ordinary employee."* So ordinary that if a billboard in the Montelli Quarter fell, she'd be crushed along with a dozen others. Zani pulled her cloak off the rack and draped it over her shoulders, just like she did every morning.

She yawned. Thankfully, none of her colleagues saw her.

She wasn't assigned too many tasks today. Mr. Alberto had said she'd be free to leave once she helped that esteemed guest in black complete {Male=his;Female=her} business at the Bank. After that, her only responsibility was to serve as {Male=his;Female=her} bodyguard and guide until the Carnevale ended. Sounded like an easy task.

*"Just do my job and I'll be good."* Zani took a deep breath. Strangely enough, she felt nervous, **her tail swishing behind her without her even realizing it.**

*"If {Male=he;Female=she} arrives at lunch time, I can suggest dining at Trattoria Margherita for Ragunna's specialities. And if {Male=he;Female=she} refuses, I'll go with the backup plan,"* Zani quickly ran through her itinerary. *"We can have pizzas at Bratta's Bakery and enjoy a relaxing afternoon by the Whisperwind Haven..."* She went over the schedule again and again, only allowing herself to relax when she couldn't find any more gaps.

A small smile tugged on the corners of her lips. There, through the bank's gate, she spotted that esteemed guest, dressed in black. With a pair of golden eyes to boot. Just as she had been expecting.

*"We've been expecting you, {PlayerName}. Welcome to Averardo Bank, {Male=sir;Female=miss}."*

The final story is the first moment. The five stories run in a loose narrative chronology — Zani's daily texture (Story I), Zani's public legend (Story II), Zani's professional reputation (Story III), Zani's private history (Story IV), Zani the morning of the Rover assignment (Story V) — and the fifth arrives at a threshold that is both professionally routine (another assignment, another scheduled meeting) and something she feels in her tail before she consciously registers it. The involuntary tail movement is the most legible sign of affect available. She is nervous. She checked the backup restaurant plan. She found no more gaps in the itinerary. She let herself smile.

The memo trails off — *"or maybe..."* — without completing the thought. The character of the item is the gap between the notebook's actual content (washing machine tutorials, forum recipes, bestseller productivity tips) and its emotional register: Zani holds onto these things because they are the infrastructure of an ordinary life that she has planned for but never quite reached. The vacation that keeps getting postponed. The free time that never arrives. The notes are small held promises that someday the day will be different. The incomplete sentence at the end is the most honest thing in the description.

The pocket watch scale of its design on an alarm clock's body is characteristic of Zani — something that functions on two registers simultaneously.

Story IV is this item's full account. The item description distills the episode to its essence: a reminder of her past, which is also a reminder of why she continues. The phrase *"having once been in a tough spot herself"* is the only available reference to Zani's own prior vulnerability — the parallel she sees in the child's eyes. **Thoughts: IV**
*"You look tired, {PlayerName}. I understand you're shouldering some heavy burdens. But I suggest you catch a break whenever you can. You don't want to end up like me. Once I pulled my first overtime, I soon found myself working around the clock. Trust me, saving the world can wait."*
The most clearly exhausted voice line. The *"you don't want to end up like me"* is said without self-pity — it is practical advice from someone who can identify the trajectory in someone else early enough to warn them, even though the warning arrived too late for herself.

**Thoughts: V**
*"I used to think life was nothing but living the same day on repeat. Grinding through trivialities, running in circles until even the smallest joys faded away. But then you came along, just as I'd grown numb to this way of living. Like the Carnevale itself, you brought a burst of color into my life."*
The Carnevale metaphor is specifically chosen: in Ragunna's Carnevale tradition, Imperator's blessing arrives as an annual festival of masks, joy, and temporary departure from ordinary life. Rover functions in this voice line the same way — not as a permanent resolution of the grinding exhaustion, but as the burst of color that makes the ordinary texture recoverable.

**Hobby**
*"I love my job! Really. No joke. Was I smiling? No? Then you get the idea."*
The format is: unambiguous positive statement → verification request → self-negating confirmation. The joke takes three beats to complete and lands via the specific rhythm of someone who has delivered this response many times in many contexts and has polished it into the most efficient possible deflection.

**Favorite Food**
*"My go-to restaurant is Trattoria Margherita. Nothing fills my stomach and soothes my soul quite like a slice of pizza freshly baked by Signorina Margherita after a long day at work."*
The distinction between filling the stomach and soothing the soul in the same sentence — both attributed to pizza — is characteristic of Zani's mode. The ordinary texture of Ragunna life is not a consolation prize. It is the actual thing she is working to protect.

**Disliked Food**
*"Employees of the Montelli family swear by espresso — straight, no sugar or milk — to keep themselves energized. But as for me, work is already hard enough... I'd rather treat myself to something sweet."*
The Disliked Food answer is a refusal of performance. The official Montelli employee drink is black espresso; she drinks something sweet instead. The sweetness is not indulgence — it is the same logic as the popsicles in the Galbrena story and the blueberry ice cream shakes: something small and deliberately pleasant to make the load lighter.

**Ideals**
*"I grew up in Ragunna, and I know every corner of this city like the back of my hand. The sea breeze that greets you, the bustling plaza filled with familiar faces, the shiny new products in shop windows, the aroma of food from Trattoria Margherita. I hope this quiet life never ends. I hope everyone finds their place. For me, that would be enough."*
The most complete statement of what Zani is actually doing this for. Not justice in an abstract sense, not Sentinel-ordained order, not the Montelli family's interests. The sea breeze. The familiar faces. The pizza smell. The hope that everyone finds their place. The quiet life that she has been fighting to protect since the second Dark Tide a decade ago, one criminal at a time, in the dark, without a name on it.

## Zani: Narrative Chronicle

- **[Ragunna — Birth and Early Life]** Zani grows up in Ragunna, Rinascita; she comes to know every corner of the city; her parents' background is not described in detail in available sources; she develops a sensitivity to the ordinary texture of Ragunna life — the sea breeze, the familiar faces, the food — that becomes the governing reason for everything she later does
- **[The Assault — Awakening (Year Unknown)]** A severely injured Zani is targeted in an attempted assault orchestrated by a redacted party (▇▇▇▇▇▇▇); during the assault she awakens her Forte — **Scorched Radiance** — and repels her assailants with newfound abilities; the Awakening triggers physical mutations: ram-like horns, a slender tail, glowing scar-marks across her upper body that emerge during Forte activation; the Resonance Spectrum Pattern cannot determine the cause of the Awakening; she is classified as a Mutant Resonator with an incubation period; the scars from this assault remain as the energy-concentration points for her Forte
- **[The Second Dark Tide — A Decade Ago]** The second Dark Tide and a Carnevale incident inflict severe damage on Ragunna; many people lose faith and form criminal gangs; Zani begins hunting them down one by one at night, delivering them to the Chamber of Discipline; order in Ragunna is eventually restored specifically because of this; the Chamber of Discipline eventually reaches a consensus that the Blazing Nightwalker is *"righteous"*; the Montelli family provides her institutional backing to manage the aftermath of her activities, which is the reason she joins them
- **[Joining the Averardo Bank]** Zani joins the Averardo Bank as a staff member; she is initially noted as *"silent, distant, a bit reckless, and a bit too comfortable with violence"*; within two months she has mastered every procedure; within two more she is on par with the bank's best employees; by her third year the Best Employee badge is routine; she has held the title for longer than she cares to count
- **[Near-Miss — Late Day]** Zani is nearly late to work during her early days, almost causing a major incident; after this she acquires the alarm clock that she has carried every day since; she has not been late since
- **[The Kidnapping Case]** Zani tracks a kidnapping ring for days; defeats the leader (a Resonator) in a separate engagement, sustaining a shoulder knife wound; infiltrates the hideout; takes down the remaining two criminals; shatters the lock on the child's cage; drapes a coat over the shaking girl; receives a toy as thanks; accepts it and promises to keep it; keeps the doll to this day as a quiet reminder of her past
- **[Ongoing — Blazing Nightwalker]** Zani continues to operate as the Blazing Nightwalker alongside her Bank duties; she has long lost count of her injuries; they heal; she is *"quite candid about the fact that this vigorous vigilante work 'changed her,' causing a 'lost spark' and a crushing exhaustion"*; her dreams are now mostly unfinished task-lists; she has made plans for free time that have not been acted on; the memo notebook's *"or maybe..."* remains incomplete
- **[Version 2.0 — Rover Arrives in Rinascita]** Zani is introduced in the main story as Rover's first contact at the Averardo Bank during the Carnevale season; she guides them through Ragunna and serves as a companion for several quests; her nervousness on the morning of that first meeting is the content of Story V
- **[Version 2.3 — Playable Character]** Zani becomes a playable character in the first phase of Version 2.3 (April 29, 2025); rerunning in Version 3.2 Phase 2
