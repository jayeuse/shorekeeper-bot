## Phrolova Profile: Basic Information

- **Full Name:** Phrolova (Chinese: 弗洛洛, Fú luò luò); the name is likely derived from the Russian patronymic root *Frol* (meaning "wise man") and the suffix *-ova* (meaning "belonging to"); the name thus carries a register of inherited or essential wisdom — a person who is constituted by knowledge; fitting for a character whose entire life has been reoriented around understanding the cycle of life and death through direct experience
- **Known Epithets:** "Discord Slayer" (external); "Fiend of Ever-burning Flame" (external); Fractsidus Overseer codename not specified in available sources — verify with Fandom Wiki
- **Identity:** Mutant Resonator; Havoc-Aligned; former senior Overseer of the Fractsidus; former violinist; sole survivor of the village of Ostina; currently residing within the Lost Beyond (a Sonoro Sphere that recreated her village); returned physically in Version 2.7
- **Affiliation:** Fractsidus (former Overseer; departed at end of Chapter II: Act XI); The Lost Beyond (self-created Sonoro Sphere)
- **Birthplace:** Ostina — an ancient village of unknown nation on Solaris-3, catastrophically destroyed by a meteor shower
- **Version Added:** Version 2.5 (July 24, 2025, first half banner); rerun in Version 2.8, Phase 1 (December 11 – December 24, 2025)
- **Rarity:** 5-Star
- **Element:** Havoc
- **Weapon:** Rectifier (wields a Lycoris [red spider lily] as a conductor's baton)
- **Forte Power:** Symphony of Beyond (original designation) / Frequency Orchestration
- **Tacet Mark Location:** Left arm (upper)
- **Post-Awakening Physical Changes:** Awakening occurred simultaneously with death at the moment of the meteor impact; the Tacet Discord mutation rendered her body ageless, with the sole visible physical difference being a faint alteration in the hue of her left iris; the mutation endows her with rapid regenerative capability — skin reconstruction in approximately 6 seconds, internal organ restoration in 15–18 minutes; at the moment of Forte awakening, Lycoris flowers grew across her body and left eye (visible in the awakening cutscene); her body houses an estimated one thousand or more discrete frequencies of absorbed beings; her right eye is perpetually bandaged — this bandage conceals the storage point of all absorbed frequencies within her body, as revealed by the Grand Architect in the Dreamcatchers in the Secret Gardens quest

## Phrolova Profile: Ontological Overview

Phrolova is the first playable character in Wuthering Waves who appears as an antagonist before becoming playable, and the first playable character to have physically died on-screen — albeit in the technically ambiguous manner that characterizes every aspect of her existence, where the boundary between death and continuation has been structurally dissolved since the meteor impact that killed her.

She was a violinist. She played an elegy for her destroyed village at a symphony hall built atop its ruins. Rover was in the audience, and recognized in her performance not a musical composition but a grief account — *her* grief account — and said so. For someone whose artistic expressions had been received as art rather than testimony, this was the first time someone had understood what she was actually doing. A friendship formed. A promise was made. Rover left without fulfilling it — then wiped their own memory, fulfilling it even less — and Phrolova, without anyone who understood her or would help her, accepted the Fractsidus's offer because they were the only entity willing to help her pursue what she needed: the resurrection of her lost people.

The relationship between her and Rover is the central personal axis of her entire story arc. It is a story of abandonment by the only person who understood her, followed by a desperate alliance with people she regarded as useful rather than trusted, followed by the exhaustion of the desperate alliance, followed by a kind of arriving-home that is also a death. She is in the Lost Beyond now, among the frequencies of the people she lost, with Hecate, surrounded by Lycoris flowers. Whether this is peace is one of the open questions Version 2.7 and beyond are still addressing.

## Phrolova — Character/Appearance Design

Phrolova's visual design is built entirely around the death-and-afterlife motif that defines her characterization: pale, still, covered in flowers associated with the world of the dead, conducting with a baton that is itself a funeral flower.

- **Hair:** Long, greyish-white; worn in two braids held with red fasteners; the hair's color reads as bleached or drained — consistent with someone who has been more dead than alive since adolescence; the braids are neat and deliberate in a way that reads as someone who maintains precise personal standards even in grief
- **Eyes:** Heterochromatic post-Awakening; the left iris has a faint hue alteration from the Tacet Discord mutation; the right eye is perpetually covered by a bandage across the right half of her face; beneath the bandage is perfectly healthy skin and a fully functioning eye — not the disfigurement viewers might expect, but the storage point for all the frequencies she houses within her body; when the bandage is removed in combat animations, her revealed appearance is visually striking precisely because it is undamaged
- **Right-face Bandage:** The bandage is not cosmetic — it is containment; the Grand Architect reveals that Phrolova's bandaged eye is the site within her body where all consumed frequencies are stored; the bandage covers the aperture of her inner empire
- **Outfit:** A red dress light pink on the underside, with a grey skirt beneath; detached sleeves; black leggings; red high-heeled shoes; the red-and-white palette is the color vocabulary of the Lycoris (red spider lily), which in Japanese and Chinese traditions is specifically the flower of the afterlife and the boundary between the living and the dead
- **Baton:** A Lycoris flower — a red spider lily — used as a conductor's baton; the gesture of conducting is her primary combat and story visual language; the choice of the death-associated flower as the instrument of her power is the most direct statement of her identity available; she waves it with *"fervent, exaggerated motions"* during the Sheet Music cherished item description's account of her practicing alone
- **Lycoris Motif:** Lycoris (higanbana) appears throughout her design at every scale — as the baton, in the flowers that grow on her body at Awakening, in the lycoris sea of the Lost Beyond, in the flower she is always seen wielding per the Game8 lore overview; the lycoris is the answer to the question of what binds her to the dead: the flower that grows at the threshold
- **Hecate:** The three-faced Tacet Discord companion who fights for Phrolova during Maestro state; derived from the Greek goddess of witchcraft, magic, and crossroads; a Calamity-class Tacet Discord from a Sonoro Sphere where all decaying frequencies converge; Hecate is Phrolova's self-identified double — *"Only when I placed my hand in its palm did I realize — Hecate is me. The me of the other world, the me of this world, and the me who moves freely between them."* Hecate's visual design reflects a three-faced, phantasmal entity whose attacks involve sharp whips and can instantly kill a target given sufficient sequential hits

## Phrolova: Psychological Profile

**Grief as Architecture**
Phrolova's entire existence after Ostina is organized around a single fact: everyone she loved is dead, and she is not. The Forte Examination Report notes approximately one thousand discrete frequencies within her body. These are not incidental — she has been specifically attempting to preserve the frequencies of the people she lost by absorbing them into Tacet Discords, then absorbing those Tacet Discords into herself. This is the Fractsidus plan in miniature applied personally: the resurrection of her parents and village via frequency preservation. It failed. The Lost Beyond was the most complete version of the attempt, and it dissolved because she could not maintain it at sufficient perfection. The Fandom Backstory page describes her as someone who converted personal tragedy into the organizing principle of an entire life; Phrolova herself would not phrase it as conversion but as consequence — she is the last container for these frequencies, and protecting them is not a project but a condition of being.

**Death as Epistemology**
Phrolova does not experience death as an endpoint. Her Chat II voice line: *"Overclocking is already common practice for the Fractsidus. I've seen countless people walk toward it, seize control, and exploit it. Of course, they are fully aware of the dangers it brings, death included. Don't disagree just yet. If you look at it from another angle, you'll see it offers not just a hopeless path to death, but a chance for a different kind of rebirth. After all, humans are creatures who seek safety, threatened by fear, clinging to life at all costs. But we... we should master not only living, but living even after death."* This is not a rationalization of dangerous practice — it is the genuine philosophical outcome of someone who died as a teenager, kept going, and has been doing so for long enough that death is simply a threshold she has passed and will pass again. Her Ideals voice line: *"I wish to comprehend the cycle of life by experiencing it over and over again."*

**Control Through Conducting**
The Fandom Backstory page identifies in Phrolova a *"secret obsession of mastering musical phrasing and dynamics skillfully, driving an orchestra to a peak where no one can escape, all in pursuit of the 'perfect form' of the symphony in her mind."* This is not separate from the frequency manipulation — it is the same impulse in two domains. She is a conductor in both senses: of music and of frequencies. The desire for the perfect form is an expression of the inability to achieve the one thing she actually needs (the return of her village) through any means currently available, redirected into the domain where perfection is at least theoretically achievable.

**Hecate as Self**
Phrolova's identification of Hecate as herself — not as a companion or a tool but as *"the me of the other world, the me of this world, and the me who moves freely between them"* — is the most direct philosophical statement in her available voice lines. Hecate is the guardian of the boundary between worlds; Phrolova is a liminal creature who has been technically dead since adolescence and lives between the world of the living and the world of her lost people. The identification is not metaphorical — it is ontological. When Phrolova says Hecate is her, she means that what Hecate is categorically (the figure who presides over the threshold) is what Phrolova is existentially.

**Practical and Discerning in Relation to Others**
The Fandom Backstory page notes that in her relations with others, Phrolova is *"practical and discerning"*; she recognized a mutual usefulness in the Grand Architect and worked with him on those terms without confusion or loyalty. She is skeptical of *"blind will"* without pragmatic grounding — *"ephemeral,"* she calls it. The Steam community discussion confirms she was open about what the Fractsidus meant to her: a benefit corporation, not an ideology. Only the Fractsidus offered her help; she accepted it without pretending it was more than it was. The departure at the end of Act XI was mutual: Schwarzloch allowed her to leave without confrontation because he viewed the overlap of their goals as inevitable regardless.

## Phrolova: Relationships

**Rover (Protagonist — The Broken Promise)**
The central relationship of Phrolova's available story. In the past — before Rover wiped their memory — Rover and Phrolova knew each other through a meaningful encounter: she performed her elegy at a symphony hall built atop her destroyed village; Rover, alone among the audience, correctly identified the performance as a grief account rather than a musical composition; she felt seen; a friendship formed; a promise was made that Rover would see her again. Rover did not. Rover wiped their own memory — erasing the promise along with the relationship — and Phrolova was left without the one person who had understood her. TV Tropes's character page: *"Phrolova joined the Fractsidus because she had nobody else to support her as the Rover purposely wiped their own memories, including a promise to Phrolova."* She calls the Rover out for this directly at the end of Dreamcatchers in the Secret Gardens: Rover condemns her for trying to resurrect her loved ones as Tacet Discords while having themselves wiped their own memories and erased every bond they formed, which Phrolova identifies as *"going far further than her in trying to achieve an impossible goal."* Her bitterness is specific and justified.

**Hecate (Companion — Tacet Discord / Self)**
The closest relationship currently in Phrolova's life. Hecate is a three-faced Calamity-class Tacet Discord from the Lost Beyond — a place where all decaying frequencies converge. She is Phrolova's proxy in combat, her companion in the Lost Beyond, and in Phrolova's self-understanding, her counterpart self: the entity that presides over thresholds and perceives all existences. Phrolova commands Hecate not because she has dominion over her in an adversarial sense but because she *is* Hecate — or Hecate is the form her boundary-crossing nature takes when externalized.

**Triss (Childhood Friend — Deceased)**
A deceased child friend from Ostina whose form the Grand Architect appropriated when attempting to manipulate Phrolova during the Fabricatorium of the Deep sequence. Phrolova told him to stop using it. The fact that she had previously *"liked"* him taking Triss's form — as the Grand Architect noted — suggests she had found some comfort in having the appearance of this friend nearby, before the manipulation became legible as what it was. Triss's role in the broader Ostina history is not documented in detail in available sources; she is specifically identified as a "child friend" whose memory the Grand Architect weaponized.

**Schwarzloch / The Grand Architect (Former Employer — Leader of the Fractsidus)**
A relationship of mutual utility acknowledged openly by both parties. Phrolova recognized him as an important point of reference but regarded his methods — the manipulation via Triss's form, the pressures to follow Cristoforo's plans before her own goals — with increasing exhaustion. She did not view the departure as defection. He accepted her departure without confrontation, noting that the eventual overlap of their objectives was inevitable. Both assessments may be accurate.

**Cristoforo (Fractsidus — Former Associate)**
Cristoforo operated as a Fractsidus member whose plans Phrolova was pressured to advance before her own objectives. The nature of their ongoing relationship in V2.7 and after is not specified in detail in available sources. Game8 notes: *"knowing Cristoforo, he is probably ecstatic and 'inspired' by the tragedy of Phrolova"* — consistent with his characterization elsewhere in the narrative.

**Angel / Galbrena (Target of Recruitment Attempt)**
The Fractsidus Wiki page notes that Schwarzloch attempted to recruit Galbrena, citing the pattern that Overseers all share the experience of *"having crossed the veil that parts life and death."* Phrolova and Galbrena do not appear to have a directly documented relationship in available sources, though both crossed that veil and both serve/served the Fractsidus in different capacities.
