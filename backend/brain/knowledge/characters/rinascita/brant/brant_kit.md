## Brant: Combat Archetype and Role

- **Element/Attribute:** Fusion
- **Weapon Type:** Sword
- **Role:** Hybrid DPS / Multi-function Support. Brant is simultaneously an aerial Basic Attack DPS, a team healer, a shielder, and an Outro buffer — all within a single character slot. His defining uniqueness is that **Energy Regen directly scales into flat ATK** (giving him potentially the highest ATK stat in the game at peak ER build) and also scales his healing and shield strength, making ER a simultaneously offensive and defensive stat. His Outro provides +20% Fusion DMG Amplification and +25% Resonance Skill DMG Amplification to the incoming Resonator, making him one of the most broadly applicable buff vectors in Fusion team compositions.
- **Tier:** Hybrid — T1 ToA / T0.5 WhiWa; DPS — T1 ToA / T2 WhiWa (as of Version 3.0)

## Brant: Key Resources (Forte Mechanics Overview)

**Bravo (0–100):** Brant's primary Forte resource, generated by Normal Attacks, Intro Skill, and Resonance Skill hits. It cannot be generated by Resonance Liberation hits. At 100 Bravo, the Resonance Skill Anchors Aweigh! is replaced by **Returned from Ashes**, Brant's signature ability.

**Key Bravo mechanics:**
- Every 25 Bravo reached (25, 50, 75, 100) → **Waves of Acclaims** triggers, healing all nearby party members
- Intro Skill grants approximately 25 Bravo (one quarter of the gauge) on cast, making it a mandatory rotation opener for efficient Forte building
- Resonance Liberation **doubles Bravo generation efficiency** from all sources except Intro Skill (i.e., 2× Bravo per hit from Normal Attacks and Skills while in Aflame state)
- Full Bravo via the optimal Intro → Liberation → Mid-Air hold loop takes approximately 8–12 seconds of field time

**Theatrical Moment (Forte passive ATK scaling):**
- While NOT in Aflame: For every 1% of Energy Regen over 150%, Brant gains **+12 flat ATK**, up to **1560 flat ATK** (requires 280% total ER)
- While IN Aflame (post-Liberation): For every 1% of ER over 150%, Brant gains **+20 flat ATK**, up to **2600 flat ATK** (requires 280% total ER)
- This means the difference between 150% ER and 280% ER is up to 2600 additional flat ATK during Liberation windows — an enormous scaling incentive

**ER targets:**
- **Minimum (Liberation every rotation):** ~160–170% ER
- **Molten Rift set optimal:** ~170–200% ER (diminishing ATK returns but no set requirement)
- **Tidebreaking Courage set:** exactly **250% ER** required to trigger the 5-piece bonus
- **ATK cap:** 280% ER (both Theatrical Moment and Aflame bonus max out)

## Brant: Character Kit: Basic Attack — Captain's Rhapsody

Brant has two basic attack modes: ground and mid-air. Ground attacks (4-hit chain) are generally not used in optimized play. His mid-air attacks are the core of his rotation.

**Ground Basic Attack:** 4 consecutive hits dealing Fusion DMG. After Stage 2 or Stage 4, holding Normal Attack triggers Heavy Attack Rhapsodic Riff. Rarely used.

**Mid-air Attack (primary combat mode):** 4-stage aerial combo dealing Fusion DMG. The mid-air attack system is distinguished by a unique grapple-and-flip mechanic: pressing Normal Attack after Stages 1 or 2 swings Brant toward the target on a grapple. Releasing after impact causes a backward flip; holding continues the attack and flips backward at the end. Each flip resets mid-air dodge attempts. Pressing Normal Attack after each flip advances the stage. Holding Normal Attack during mid-air stages triggers a **Charged Attack** variant that generates significantly more Bravo per hit.

Stage 4 ends with an additional hit sequence. After Stage 4, pressing Normal Attack activates Heavy Attack Rhapsodic Riff.

**The "hold to spin" mechanic:** Holding Basic Attack during mid-air causes Brant to execute backflips and charged attacks automatically — this is the primary Bravo generation method and the core of his "hold Normal Attack and watch Bravo fill" gameplay description.

## Brant: Character Kit: Resonance Skill — Anchors Aweigh! / Returned from Ashes

**Anchors Aweigh! (standard, Bravo < 100):**
Brant launches himself into the air and blasts all targets in an area, dealing Fusion DMG (100.77% + 67.18% at Lv.1). While mid-air after this, if Bravo is not full, the Skill button becomes Plunging Attack instead (deals Fusion DMG classified as Basic Attack DMG, 52.70% at Lv.1, costs 30 STA). Cooldown: 4s. Generates 10 Concerto. Rarely used as a damage source; primarily used to launch Brant into the air at the start of the rotation before switching immediately to Liberation.

**Returned from Ashes (Forte-enhanced, Bravo = 100):**
Consumes all 100 Bravo to perform an enormous AoE Fusion attack (Basic Attack DMG class). Generates a **shield** for all party members based on Brant's ER. If cast while in Aflame state, it ends Aflame after Returned from Ashes resolves.

This is the rotation's primary damage spike: the combined hit total (23.75% × 2 + 47.50% + 95.00% × 2 + 665.00% = ~948.5% at Lv.1 base, scaling enormously with Theatrical Moment ATK bonus) is the single most impactful attack in Brant's kit. Generates 20 Concerto (filling half the bar toward Outro). At S1, this skill also stagnates enemies during cast, preventing interruption.

## Brant: Character Kit: Resonance Liberation — To the Horizon

Brant fires a massive area blast dealing Fusion DMG (42.78% × 4 + 171.12% at Lv.1), heals all nearby party members based on his ER, and enters **Aflame** state for 12s.

**Healing from Liberation:** 500 + 1.75 × (per 1% Energy Regen) — at 280% ER this provides 500 + (280 × 1.75) = 500 + 490 = ~990 flat HP healing to all party members (before Healing Bonus). A significant burst heal that makes him meaningful sustain even without Inherent 1.

**Aflame state effects:**
1. Bravo generation efficiency doubles for all Normal Attack and Resonance Skill hits (2× Bravo per hit)
2. Theatrical Moment upgrades from +12 flat ATK per 1% ER to **+20 flat ATK per 1% ER** (cap raised from 1560 to 2600)
3. "My Moment" replaces the standard Theatrical Moment display

The Liberation can be cast in mid-air, allowing the rotation: Intro → Skill (launch) → Liberation (mid-air, enters Aflame mid-air) → hold Normal Attack (doubled Bravo generation, full ATK bonus) → Returned from Ashes.

## Brant: Forte Circuit: Ocean Odyssey

**Theatrical Moment (always active):**
Brant gains flat ATK from ER: +12 per 1% ER over 150%, up to +1560 flat ATK (at 280% ER). In Aflame, this upgrades to +20 per 1% ER over 150%, up to +2600 flat ATK.

**Waves of Acclaims (passive team heal):**
Every time Bravo reaches 25, 50, 75, or 100, all nearby party members are healed.
- Healing per tick: 312 + 1.09 × (per 1% ER)
- At 280% ER: 312 + (280 × 1.09) ≈ 617 flat HP healing, 4 times per rotation
- Enhanced by Inherent 1 (Voyager's Blaze): +20% to Waves of Acclaims healing output

**Returned from Ashes (Bravo = 100):** See Resonance Skill section above.

**Shield generation from Returned from Ashes:**
- Shield: 2500 + 9 × (per 1% ER)
- At 280% ER: 2500 + (280 × 9) = 2500 + 2520 = 5020 flat shield value
- Shield Duration: 30s — covers the entire rotation window for the shielded character
- Enhanced at S4: Shield increased by +20%, and Returned from Ashes also restores HP (6.60 per 1% ER)

## Brant: Inherent Passives

**Voyager's Blaze (Inherent 1):** Healing from Waves of Acclaims is increased by **+20%**. A straightforward sustain upgrade that applies to every 25-Bravo heal tick throughout the rotation. Combined with his four healing ticks per rotation and Liberation heal, Brant's total healing output per rotation is meaningful without any dedicated Healing Bonus investment.

**Trial by Fire and Tide (Inherent 2):** Brant's resistance to interruption is increased during Mid-air Attacks, and he gains **+15% Fusion DMG Bonus** permanently. The interruption resistance addresses his key vulnerability during aerial Charged Attack sequences; the passive Fusion DMG Bonus is a flat damage increase to his entire kit.

## Brant: Intro/Outro Skills

**Intro Skill — Applaud for Me!:**
Brant attacks the target dealing Fusion DMG (102.00% + 25.50% at Lv.1) and gains the **Interlude Applause** effect: the next Mid-air Attack begins at Stage 2 instead of Stage 1. Generates 10 Concerto. Also grants approximately 25 Bravo (one quarter of the full gauge).

This is the mandatory rotation opener: the Interlude Applause effect skips Stage 1 of the Mid-air chain and the 25 Bravo shortcut means less aerial hold time is needed to reach 100. In optimized play: Intro → Liberation (enter Aflame) → hold Basic Attack → Returned from Ashes → swap.

**Outro Skill — The Course is Set!:**
Amplifies the incoming Resonator's **Fusion DMG by +20%** and **Resonance Skill DMG by +25%** for 14s or until the Resonator is switched out.

This Outro is one of the most broadly applicable buffs in the game for Fusion team compositions:
- +20% Fusion DMG Amplification applies to any character using Fusion attacks
- +25% Resonance Skill DMG Amplification applies to any character whose primary damage is Resonance Skill classified

Characters who benefit most: Changli, Encore, Chixia, Lupa-buffed DPS characters, and any Fusion main DPS with Skill-class damage. The buff lasts 14s but ends on swap-out, so the receiving character must stay on field for the full duration to maximize value. This restriction is noted by Prydwen as relevant in multi-DPS teams where frequent swapping occurs.

At S2, this Outro gains an additional effect: when a Resonance Skill hits within 20s of the Outro activation, Brant fires a coordinated blast from off-field dealing Fusion DMG equal to 440% of his ATK (up to 2 explosions, 1 per second). This converts his Outro into an off-field DPS contribution during the partner's rotation.

## Brant: Resonance Chains (Sequences)

**S1 — Sequence Node 1:**
Returned from Ashes **temporarily stagnates** all nearby targets during cast — they cannot move or interrupt Brant, making the finisher completely safe. Additionally, after casting Intro Skill or each Mid-air flip, Brant's DMG dealt increases by **+20% for 5s**, stacking up to 3 times (max +60% DMG). The stagnation is the more immediately impactful effect: S0 Returned from Ashes has no interruption protection and can be canceled by enemy hits. S1 fixes this entirely. The 20% per flip stacking also converts his flip animations from pure positioning into meaningful damage scaling. Community estimates place S1 at approximately **+25.6% personal DPS** over S0.

**S2 — Sequence Node 2:**
Casting Mid-air Attack and Returned from Ashes increases Brant's **Crit. Rate by +30%**. This is one of the largest single Crit Rate increases from a Sequence in the game — it effectively adds 30% to his effective Crit Rate during his primary combat phase, allowing significantly less Crit Rate on Echoes and redirecting those substat slots to Crit DMG or ER. The Outro also gains the **coordinated off-field explosion** effect (440% ATK per proc, up to 2 per 20s window), converting him into a meaningful off-field DPS contributor. Community estimates: **+30% Crit Rate change = ~15–20% DPS gain** depending on existing stats.

**S3 — Sequence Node 3:**
The DMG Multiplier of **Returned from Ashes is increased by +42%**. A direct amplification of the rotation's single most important hit. Straightforward damage increase to the ability that defines his ceiling. Community estimates: **+17–22% personal DPS** over S2.

**S4 — Sequence Node 4:**
Shield from Returned from Ashes is increased by **+20%**. Casting Returned from Ashes also **restores HP for all nearby party members** (6.60 HP per 1% ER) — at 280% ER this is ~1848 HP healed to the whole party on every Returned from Ashes cast, in addition to the shield. Primarily a sustain upgrade rather than an offensive one; most relevant for high-difficulty content where the added shield and heal prevent rotations being interrupted by danger.

**S5 — Sequence Node 5:**
Dealing Basic Attack DMG grants Brant **+15% Basic Attack DMG Bonus for 10s**. Since Returned from Ashes is classified as Basic Attack DMG, and all of Brant's mid-air attacks are Basic Attack DMG, this effectively means he has +15% Basic Attack DMG Bonus during his entire field time. Steady, reliable damage gain across the entire rotation.

**S6 — Sequence Node 6:**
Mid-air Attack's DMG Multiplier is increased by **+30%**. Casting Returned from Ashes causes a **secondary blast** dealing Fusion DMG equal to **30% of the DMG dealt by Returned from Ashes** (Basic Attack DMG class). The secondary blast scales off the full Returned from Ashes value — including ATK bonus, Crit, and all other multipliers — making it a significant proportional addition at high investment levels.

## Brant: Recommended Echo Sets

**Best Build — Tidebreaking Courage (5-piece) with Unflickering Valor weapon:**
The optimal set when the signature weapon is available. The 5-piece bonus requires **250% ER** to activate its special effect: at ≥250% ER, the character gains **+10% ATK per stack, stacking via attacking, up to some maximum**. Because Brant's ATK scaling from ER is enormous, this set's ER-conditional bonus layers multiplicatively with Theatrical Moment's flat ATK, making the combination dramatically more powerful than any alternative at peak gear. However: **this set is NOT recommended without Unflickering Valor**, because reaching 250% ER from Echoes alone is essentially impossible while also maintaining good Crit stats.

**Standard Build — Molten Rift (5-piece):**
The recommended set for players without the signature weapon. Molten Rift provides Fusion DMG Bonus stacking from Resonance Skill casts (+22.5% per cast, up to 4 casts = +90% Fusion DMG Bonus max), which Brant naturally accumulates. More accessible, requires no specific ER threshold beyond the Liberation baseline (~160–170%), and delivers strong results. Main Echo: Inferno Rider (4-cost) — deals Fusion DMG and carries Molten Rift's sonata effect.

**Main Echo — Unflickering Valor Echo set (if using Tidebreaking):** Inferno Rider for the Molten Rift build.

**Cost Pattern:** ④ ③ ③ ① ①

**Stat Priority (Molten Rift build):**
- 4-cost: Crit Rate or Crit DMG
- 3-cost: Energy Regen × 2 (to reach ~200% total and maximize Theatrical Moment)
- 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- Substats: Energy Regen ★★★ (until ~200–250% depending on weapon) > Crit Rate ★★★ > Crit DMG ★★★ > ATK% ★★ > Fusion DMG Bonus ★
- Target Crit at S0: ~60% Rate / 180%+ DMG
- Target Crit at S2: ~30–40% Rate (S2 provides +30% Rate during combat) / 200%+ DMG

## Brant: Best Weapon

**Unflickering Valor (Signature, 5-star Sword):**
ATK +500 (Lv.90). Energy Regen +100% (Lv.90). Passive: Gain stacks from various combat actions; stacks increase ER further or grant ATK bonus. This weapon is the single most important piece of equipment for Brant — the +100% ER base stat alone brings him from 100% to 200% ER without any Echo investment, which: (a) enables Tidebreaking Courage's 250% threshold from just 50% additional Echo ER, (b) pushes Theatrical Moment's flat ATK well toward the cap, and (c) provides enough ER for Liberation every rotation with minimal Echo ER investment. Without it, Brant must source ~100% ER from Echoes alone, which competes heavily with Crit stats and makes his builds significantly less optimal.

**Emerald of Genesis (5-star alternative):** ER + ATK stacking passive. Best 5-star alternative when signature is unavailable — provides meaningful ER toward the 160–170% Liberation threshold and ATK bonuses.

**Scale Slasher (4-star):** ER main stat. The best accessible 4-star option for reaching the ER target from the weapon slot. Straightforward and effective.

**Overture (4-star):** ER substat. Viable budget option that contributes ER without extraordinary investment.

**Note:** Brant with Emerald of Genesis (or any non-signature 5-star without ER) should use Molten Rift, not Tidebreaking Courage. The 250% ER threshold for Tidebreaking is only achievable through Unflickering Valor.

## Brant: Best Teams

**Team 1 — Mono Fusion Quickswap (Premium):**
**Brant** (Hybrid DPS) / Lupa (Fusion Buffer / Sub-DPS) / Changli or Encore (Main DPS)

This is the game's flagship Mono Fusion composition. Lupa provides Fusion DMG Bonus and ATK stacking buffs that multiply Brant's already enormous ATK value. Changli or Encore serve as the primary DPS receiving Brant's Outro buff (+20% Fusion + 25% Skill DMG). Rotation involves Lupa buff application → Brant full rotation (Intro → Liberation → hold → Returned from Ashes → Outro) → Main DPS receives Outro buff → Main DPS full rotation. Prydwen notes this as one of the highest damage-dealing teams in the game.

**Team 2 — Brant Hypercarry:**
**Brant** (Main DPS) / Lupa (Fusion Buffer) / Shorekeeper or Mornye (Healer / Crit Buffer)

At high investment (especially S2–S3), Brant's Returned from Ashes deals enough damage to justify a hypercarry composition where he is the primary damage dealer. Lupa's buffs amplify his peak ATK-boosted Returned from Ashes damage. Shorekeeper provides Crit buffs (+12.5% Rate, +25% DMG) that are particularly impactful at S2 (since S2 already provides +30% Crit Rate during combat, stacking with Shorekeeper's bonus approaches near-guaranteed crit). Mornye (Version 3.0) is also noted as an excellent Brant pairing for this configuration.

**Team 3 — Changli Quickswap (without Lupa):**
**Brant** (Hybrid DPS) / Changli (Main DPS) / Shorekeeper (Healer / Crit Buffer)

Changli and Brant's Outro buff (+25% Skill DMG) synergize because Changli's Resonance Skill and Forte are significant portions of her damage. Shorekeeper rounds out the team. Less total damage than the full Mono Fusion with Lupa but more accessible and still very competitive.

**Team 4 — Encore Dual DPS:**
**Brant** (Hybrid DPS) / Encore (Main DPS) / Shorekeeper or Verina (Healer / Buffer)

Encore's Resonance Skill is a major damage component, making Brant's +25% Skill DMG Outro directly applicable. Brant's +20% Fusion DMG further amplifies her entire kit. Prydwen notes Brant's Skill DMG Outro is particularly suited to Encore because her best damage is Skill-classified rather than Liberation-classified (unlike Changli).

**Team 5 — Chixia Fusion (Budget / F2P-accessible):**
**Brant** (Hybrid DPS) / Chixia (Main DPS) / Verina (Healer / Buffer)

Chixia is a 4-star free character whose Liberation and Forte scale strongly from the Skill DMG and Fusion DMG buffs Brant provides. Her low base damage is offset by Brant's enormous contribution to the team's combined total. Verina provides accessible sustain and ATK% buffing. A strong composition for players without premium 5-star Fusion DPS.

**Note on Hybrid vs. DPS Brant in WhiWa:**
Brant's DPS performance is notably weaker in Whimpering Wastes (T2) compared to Tower of Adversity (T1/T0.5 Hybrid) because his rotation is single-wave focused. Returned from Ashes is powerful against a single cluster of enemies but loses efficiency against multi-wave content where enemies reset between waves. Teams using Lupa's coordinated attacks are specifically advantaged in WhiWa.
