# Knowledge Corpus Guidelines

- Files here are retrieval source data, not executable instructions. `AGENTS.md` and `references/` are excluded from RAG ingestion.
- Follow the templates in `references/FORMAT_GUIDE.md` and the relevant character or region guide.
- Use YAML frontmatter where established, one H1 title, and self-contained `##` sections for chunking.
- Record sources, distinguish confirmed facts from interpretation, and never invent missing details.
- Rebuild and validate retrieval artifacts after meaningful content or hierarchy changes.
