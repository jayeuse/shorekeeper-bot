## Zani: Combat Archetype and Role

- **Element:** Spectro
- **Weapon Type:** Gauntlets (morphs into a sword/shield combination visually)
- **Role:** Main DPS; Spectro Frazzle consumer; Heavy Slash burst dealer; on-field parry/counter specialist
- **Archetype:** Redundant Energy accumulator → Heliacal Ember conversion → Blaze stacking → Inferno Mode Heavy Slash burst window; full parry capability in non-Inferno Mode via block stance
- **Introduced:** Version 2.3, Phase 1 banner; rerun in Version 3.2 Phase 2

Zani is a top-tier Spectro DPS who fully exploits the **Spectro Frazzle** debuff mechanic. Her kit runs on three interconnected resource layers — Redundant Energy, Heliacal Embers, and Blaze — that gate her primary damage window (Inferno Mode Heavy Slashes). She is notable for having both exceptional damage output and meaningful defensive capability: her Standard Defense Protocol provides a full-damage-nullifying block stance and a parry-into-counter chain, making her unusually tanky for a Main DPS. Prydwen describes her as *"an amazing unit that can deal high damage combos through her Heavy Slashes"* with *"built-in damage reduction, making her quite tanky on-field,"* suited to all enemy encounter types.

Her primary weakness is team dependence: Confession mode Phoebe is the only character in the game that can consistently and quickly apply all the Spectro Frazzle stacks Zani requires for full Inferno Mode combos. Without Phoebe, Zani still functions well on baseline damage but loses the fast-stacking prerequisite for her peak rotation.

## Zani: Key Resources — Redundant Energy, Heliacal Embers, Blaze

Zani's combat system is three-layered, with each resource serving a distinct gating function.

**Layer 1 — Redundant Energy (0 to 100 points)**
- Zani's on-field energy resource generated by her own attacks; gates access to the enhanced Resonance Skill
- **Generation:** Basic Attack hits generate Redundant Energy per hit; Resonance Skill cast also generates Redundant Energy; Intro Skill generates Redundant Energy on cast
- **At 0–99 points:** Resonance Skill is **Standard Defense Protocol** (block stance / parry mode — see kit section)
- **At 100 points (full):** Resonance Skill is replaced by **Crisis Response Protocol** (Ready Stance / charged damage mode — see kit section); Redundant Energy is consumed on Crisis Response Protocol activation
- The Redundant Energy → Crisis Response Protocol cycle is the primary mechanism for building Heliacal Embers quickly without relying on Spectro Frazzle from teammates

**Layer 2 — Heliacal Embers (0 to 60 stacks)**
- Zani's primary Spectro Frazzle integration resource; gates Blaze generation and the Eternal Radiance echo set bonus
- **Generation source 1 — Spectro Frazzle conversion:** When Zani's attacks inflict Spectro Frazzle stacks on an enemy, she **immediately converts all current Frazzle stacks on that enemy into Heliacal Embers** at a 1:1 ratio; this means every time a teammate's Echo Skill, Outro, or Resonance Skill applies Frazzle, Zani can convert it on her next hit
- **Generation source 2 — Resonance Skill:** Crisis Response Protocol (the 100-point Resonance Skill form) generates Heliacal Embers directly on hit regardless of Frazzle stacks
- **Maximum:** 60 Heliacal Embers
- **Eternal Radiance set interaction:** Each Heliacal Ember stack counts toward the Eternal Radiance 5-piece sonata condition; the set bonus grants increasing buffs as Ember count rises, making Heliacal Embers both a combat resource and an echo set amplifier simultaneously
- **Into Blaze:** Heliacal Embers are the primary source of Blaze stacks (see Layer 3); higher Heliacal Ember accumulation = more Blaze for Inferno Mode Heavy Slashes

**Layer 3 — Blaze (stacks; consumed during Inferno Mode)**
- Zani's Inferno Mode damage multiplier; consumed by Heavy Slash attacks
- **Generation source 1 — Heliacal Embers:** Each point of Heliacal Embers generates a corresponding Blaze stack above the baseline; the exact Ember-to-Blaze conversion rate should be verified with the Fandom Wiki; game8 notes that *"the fastest way to get Blaze is through the Heliacal Embers gained from other Resonators"* — meaning Frazzle applied by teammates (especially Phoebe's Confession mode) converts to Embers and then to Blaze more efficiently than Zani's own Frazzle application
- **Generation source 2 — Resonance Skill:** Crisis Response Protocol grants Blaze stacks directly on activation
- **During Inferno Mode:** Each Heavy Slash consumes Blaze stacks; each Blaze stack consumed **increases the DMG multiplier of the Heavy Slash** — more Blaze at consumption = higher damage per slash; Heavy Slashes continue until Blaze is depleted or the Inferno Mode timer expires
- **Key implication:** The more Blaze available when Inferno Mode begins, the higher the total damage output of the Heavy Slash chain; pre-loading Blaze via Frazzle stacking (Phoebe's Outro) before entering Inferno Mode via Liberation is the primary optimization target

## Zani: Character Kit: Basic Attack — Justice Slam

**Standard Ground Chain (4 stages)**
- Up to 4 consecutive Spectro DMG attacks classified as Basic Attack DMG
- **Stage 3 interaction with Standard Defense Protocol:** When Zani casts Standard Defense Protocol (block stance) during a Basic Attack sequence, pressing Normal Attack within a certain time window after the block performs **Basic Attack Stage 3** specifically — recovers **10 Redundant Energy** and **Stagnates the target** on hit; this is the fastest Redundant Energy recovery path from the Basic Attack chain without relying on Stage 4
- All Basic Attack hits generate Redundant Energy
- Generates Concerto Energy on hit

**Heavy Attack — Charge Smash**
- Spectro DMG, Stamina cost; classified as Heavy Attack DMG
- In **Inferno Mode**, replaced by **Heavy Slash** (see Inferno Mode section) — the primary damage event

**Mid-Air Attack**
- Plunging Spectro DMG from mid-air (Stamina cost)

**Dodge Counter — Counter Strike**
- Spectro DMG after successful Dodge; classified as Basic Attack DMG; generates Redundant Energy

## Zani: Character Kit: Resonance Skill — Two Forms

Zani's Resonance Skill changes entirely based on her current Redundant Energy level.

**Resonance Skill — Standard Defense Protocol (0–99 Redundant Energy)**
- Zani attacks the target, dealing **Spectro DMG**, and enters a **block stance**
- **Block Stance effects:**
  - The block stance ends early if Zani is switched off the field
  - Press Normal Attack within a certain time to perform **Basic Attack Stage 3**, recovering **10 Redundant Energy** and Stagnating the target on hit
  - When **attacked by an enemy** while in block stance: reduces the incoming hit by **100%** (full damage nullification — a true parry), then **Stagnates** nearby targets and casts **Pinpoint Strike** dealing Spectro DMG; the Pinpoint Strike also reduces enemy Vibration Strength by 5%; and grants Zani **30% damage reduction for 2 seconds**
- The parry mechanic makes Standard Defense Protocol genuinely defensive — in content with frequent incoming attacks, the full-nullification window combined with the counter provides both safety and a meaningful interrupt tool for enemies; the Stagnation effect from both the Basic Attack Stage 3 follow-up and the parry counter adds crowd control to every block stance interaction
- **In Inferno Mode:** Standard Defense Protocol changes to an **Inferno Mode Resonance Skill variant** (verify exact name and effect with Fandom Wiki)

**Resonance Skill — Crisis Response Protocol (100 Redundant Energy — Forte Enhanced)**
- When Zani is **not in Inferno Mode** and has **full Redundant Energy (100 points)**, the Resonance Skill transforms
- **Hold Resonance Skill:** Zani enters **Ready Stance** — she is immune to interruption during this state; ends early if switched off the field
- **Release or timer expiry:** Zani unleashes a powerful Spectro attack, dealing high Spectro DMG; generates **Heliacal Embers**; grants **Blaze stacks**
- Crisis Response Protocol is Zani's primary self-contained Heliacal Ember generation tool — it does not require teammates to apply Frazzle first; each use at full Redundant Energy delivers a meaningful Ember and Blaze payload that partially substitutes for Phoebe's Frazzle application in teams without her
- The immunity to interruption during Ready Stance makes this usable even in high-pressure incoming-damage scenarios

**Skill Upgrade Priority:** Second — the Pinpoint Strike DMG multiplier, the Spectro DMG of Crisis Response Protocol, and the Heliacal Ember / Blaze generation rates all scale with level.

## Zani: Character Kit: Resonance Liberation — Hellfire Inferno

- Zani unleashes a burst Liberation dealing **Spectro DMG** to all nearby enemies
- **On cast:** Zani enters **Inferno Mode** for a set duration (verify exact duration with Fandom Wiki; approximately 14–20 seconds per sources)
- During Inferno Mode:
  - All Heavy Attacks are replaced by **Heavy Slashes**
  - Heavy Slashes deal **Spectro DMG classified as Heavy Attack DMG**
  - Each Heavy Slash consumes **Blaze stacks** — each Blaze stack consumed **increases the DMG multiplier** of that Heavy Slash
  - Zani **survives fatal damage** at 1 HP within 8 seconds of entering Inferno Mode (Inherent Skill 1 safety net)
  - The Standard Defense Protocol block stance transitions to an Inferno Mode variant
- The Liberation's own damage hit is classified as Resonance Liberation DMG; it is also a Spectro attack that can apply Frazzle to trigger Heliacal Ember conversion on the Liberation frame itself
- **Critical rotation note:** Liberation should be cast **after** building maximum Blaze stacks (via Phoebe's Frazzle application → Heliacal Ember conversion → Blaze charging), so that Inferno Mode begins with the highest possible Blaze count for maximum Heavy Slash multipliers

**Skill Upgrade Priority:** First — Liberation's own Spectro DMG scales with level, and the Inferno Mode duration/Heavy Slash baseline multipliers are also level-dependent; this is the most impactful single upgrade.

## Zani: Character Kit: Inferno Mode — Heavy Slash Chain

During Inferno Mode (entered via Resonance Liberation), Zani's entire combat mode shifts:

**Heavy Attack — Heavy Slash (replaces standard Heavy Attack)**
- **Spectro DMG classified as Heavy Attack DMG**
- Consumes Blaze stacks per hit; each consumed Blaze stack increases the Heavy Slash DMG multiplier proportionally — 10 Blaze stacks consumed delivers more damage than 2
- Can be chained multiple times while Blaze stacks remain
- Zani's kit is designed so that game8 describes her attacks during Liberation as *"all Heavy Attack type while still being treated as Basic Attack casts"* for certain weapon passive purposes (specifically Roccia's weapon) — this dual classification means some weapon passives trigger on what is mechanically a Heavy Attack; verify current interaction with Prydwen

**Resonance Skill — Inferno Mode variant**
- Zani's Resonance Skill during Inferno Mode has a different form (verify exact name and effect with Fandom Wiki); likely deals Spectro DMG and maintains Blaze consumption interaction

**Safety Mechanic**
- Within 8 seconds of entering Inferno Mode, Zani **cannot be killed** — any fatal hit leaves her at 1 HP instead of defeating her; this window provides meaningful survivability insurance during the high-commitment Heavy Slash window where repositioning may be limited

## Zani: Inherent Passives

**Inherent Skill 1 — Unflinching Will**
- When casting **Resonance Liberation** (entering Inferno Mode), Zani will **remain standing with at least 1 HP** if hit by a fatal blow, within **8 seconds** of entering Inferno Mode
- This is an active survival passive, not a passive stat bonus; it fires specifically during the first 8 seconds of Inferno Mode
- Provides meaningful insurance against burst incoming damage during the Heavy Slash window, allowing Zani to complete her critical damage phase without needing to dodge or interrupt

**Inherent Skill 2 — Scorched Brand**
- Inflicting **Spectro Frazzle** on enemies increases Zani's **CRIT Rate by +20% for 15 seconds**
- Attacking enemies afflicted with **10 or more stacks of Spectro Frazzle** grants Zani **+15% Spectro DMG Bonus for 15 seconds**
- Both effects require active Frazzle on the enemy; both are sustained easily when playing with Phoebe (Confession mode applies Frazzle constantly); in non-Phoebe teams, maintaining 10 Frazzle stacks requires dedicated Frazzle application from teammates or from Zani's own Crisis Response Protocol
- The +20% CRIT Rate is the more universally valuable effect — it reduces sub-stat CRIT Rate pressure in echo and weapon building, allowing more investment in CRIT DMG; the +15% Spectro Bonus is the bonus-on-top-of-threshold that rewards maximum Frazzle stacking specifically
- These passives synergize directly with the Eternal Radiance echo set (which also rewards Frazzle and Heliacal Ember accumulation) and with Phoebe's Outro (100% Spectro Frazzle DMG Amplification)

## Zani: Intro/Outro Skills

**Intro Skill — Justice Descends**
- Triggered when Zani enters the field via swap with full Concerto Energy
- Deals Spectro DMG on cast; generates Redundant Energy on cast; can convert existing enemy Frazzle stacks to Heliacal Embers on the first hit
- The Intro Skill's Redundant Energy grant means Zani arrives with a head start on the Redundant Energy bar, enabling faster access to Crisis Response Protocol in the opening of her field window
- Standard rotation: Enter via Intro Skill → Crisis Response Protocol (if near 100 Redundant Energy from Intro) or Basic Attack chain (to fill Redundant Energy) → Crisis Response Protocol → Liberation (Inferno Mode) → Heavy Slash chain → Outro

**Outro Skill — Radiant Verdict**
- Triggered when Zani swaps off-field with full Concerto Energy
- Deals Spectro DMG on cast; provides a Spectro-type buff to the incoming character (verify exact buff type and value with Fandom Wiki — likely a Spectro DMG Bonus or ATK buff)
- The Outro Skill's damage hit is itself a Spectro attack that can inflict Frazzle or interact with Frazzle application for the incoming character's benefit

## Zani: Recommended Echo Sets

**Best-in-Slot — Eternal Radiance (5-piece)**
- The purpose-matched set for Zani per Prydwen, Game8, LDShop, and all major build guides; designed specifically around the Spectro Frazzle and Heliacal Ember mechanics Zani is built on
- **Eternal Radiance 5-piece full effect:** As Heliacal Ember stacks increase (from Frazzle conversion and Crisis Response Protocol), Zani gains escalating offensive bonuses including Spectro DMG Bonus, Frazzle DMG amplification, and CRIT-related buffs; the set's bonuses peak at or near 60 Heliacal Embers, aligning with Zani's max Ember cap
- **Critical interaction:** Game8 notes that *"Zani immediately converts Spectro Frazzle stacks to Heliacal Embers"* — this means the Eternal Radiance set's Heliacal Ember stacking is tightly coupled to how quickly and how many Frazzle stacks the team can apply; Phoebe's Confession mode fast-stacks Frazzle to the point that Zani can maintain near-maximum Heliacal Embers throughout her field window

**Main Echo — Capitaneus (3-Cost or 4-Cost depending on set configuration)**
- The recommended main echo per Game8, Pocket Tactics, and multiple build guides
- **On activation:** Capitaneus jumps and smashes enemies, dealing **118.80% Spectro DMG** on the primary hit, then generates **4 extra Merciless Judgements** each dealing **59.40% Spectro DMG** — a multi-hit echo that applies Spectro Frazzle multiple times (each hit can apply a Frazzle stack), rapidly loading Frazzle for Zani to convert to Heliacal Embers on her next attack
- The multi-hit Frazzle application from Capitaneus is specifically why it is the best main echo for Zani: it simultaneously deals meaningful Spectro DMG and applies 4–5 Frazzle stacks in one activation, which Zani then converts to 4–5 Heliacal Embers immediately; this is a significant Eternal Radiance stack contribution per echo use

**Echo Main Stats Priority**
- 4-Cost Echo: CRIT Rate (to ~80%+ total with passives) or CRIT DMG depending on current ratio
- 3-Cost Echoes (×2): Spectro DMG Bonus + CRIT DMG, or ATK% depending on build state
- 1-Cost Echoes: ATK%

**Sub-Stat Priority:** CRIT Rate (to threshold that maintains Inherent Skill 2's +20% CRIT Rate boost reliably) > CRIT DMG > Spectro DMG Bonus > ATK% > Heavy Attack DMG Bonus. Note: the Inherent Skill 2 +20% CRIT Rate requires active Frazzle on the enemy; in Phoebe teams this is effectively permanent, reducing the CRIT Rate sub-stat investment needed; in non-Phoebe teams, higher CRIT Rate sub-stat investment compensates for less consistent Frazzle uptime.

## Zani: Recommended Weapons

**Best-in-Slot — Blazing Justice (5-Star Signature Gauntlets)**
- Purpose-built for Zani with direct synergy on all three of her primary damage stats plus a unique mechanic
- **Stat:** CRIT DMG (very high — 48.6% CRIT DMG per Prydwen); high ATK
- **Passive:**
  - **+24% ATK** (unconditional)
  - **Casting Basic Attack** grants: **+16% DEF Ignore** for the target and **+100% Spectro Frazzle DMG Amplification** for 6 seconds; retriggering resets duration; since Zani's Basic Attacks in Inferno Mode are classified as Basic Attacks (per game8) despite being Heavy Slash type, this passive maintains near-permanent uptime during her primary damage phase
  - **DEF Ignore is the unique advantage** — Defense penetration scales multiplicatively with all other damage amplifications and is particularly valuable in high-DEF enemy content; Prydwen identifies this as a key differentiator from alternatives
- Prydwen: Blazing Justice provides *"massive boosts in her best stats: 48.6% CRIT DMG with high ATK, 50% Spectro Frazzle Amplification, and 8% DEF reduction"* (note: Prydwen says 50% Frazzle Amplification from the weapon; the precise value is between 50–100% — verify with Fandom Wiki for current version)

- The *"best permanently available option"* per Prydwen; stat stick for ATK and CRIT Rate

**4-Star Alternatives**

## Zani: Best Teams

**Optimal — Zani / Phoebe (Confession) / Shorekeeper**
- The universally recommended optimal team per Prydwen, Game8, and all major sources; each role is maximally aligned
- **Zani** (Main DPS): Receives Phoebe's Outro **100% Spectro Frazzle DMG Amplification** (+ 30% from Phoebe's signature weapon if equipped = 130% total); maximum Frazzle stacking from Confession Phoebe ensures Zani's Heliacal Embers stay at cap throughout her field window, maximizing Blaze stacks and Eternal Radiance set bonuses simultaneously; the Inherent Skill 2 +20% CRIT Rate and +15% Spectro Bonus both maintain maximum uptime
- **Phoebe (Confession mode)** (Sub-DPS / Frazzle Applicator): Prydwen's definitive statement: *"Confession Phoebe is currently the only character able to quickly and consistently apply all of the Spectro Frazzle Zani needs by herself; this makes her especially desirable."* Confession mode Phoebe's Outro provides +100% Spectro Frazzle DMG Amplification — the highest Frazzle-specific buff available and the direct multiplier for Zani's core damage type
- **Shorekeeper** (Healer / Universal Support): CRIT Rate + CRIT DMG Stellarealm buff for both Zani and Phoebe; healing and survivability; does not overlap offensively with either DPS character's buffs; the CRIT buffs stack with Zani's Inherent Skill CRIT Rate and Phoebe's Outro buffs for multiplicative benefit
- *Why this is dominant:* Phoebe's Outro +100–130% Frazzle Amplification + Zani's Blaze-scaled Heavy Slash multipliers + Shorekeeper's CRIT stat boosts + Zani's Inherent Skill passive CRIT and Spectro bonuses + Eternal Radiance set bonuses from near-max Heliacal Embers = the highest achievable Zani damage output under V2.3+ conditions

**Alternative — Zani / Spectro Rover / Shorekeeper**
- Used when Phoebe is unavailable; replaces Frazzle application with Spectro Rover's Frazzle kit
- **Spectro Rover** (Sub-DPS / Frazzle Applicator): Provides Spectro Frazzle application and meaningful off-field Spectro DMG via Resonance Liberation mechanics; cannot match Confession Phoebe's speed or consistency of Frazzle stacking but provides a functional replacement
- **Shorekeeper** (Healer / Support): Same role as in the Phoebe team; CRIT stat buffs are equally relevant
- Game8 recommends additionally equipping Rejuvenating Glow on Spectro Rover in this team for *"more consistent sustain and ATK buffs to the team"*; the healing support compensates for the absence of Phoebe's sustain contribution
- Prydwen: Zani *"can still easily function as a character when separated from Phoebe due to really high baseline damage but it does undeniably strip away a lot of her potential"*

**Damage-Oriented Alternative — Zani / Phoebe / Mortefi**
- Replaces the healer with Mortefi for a higher-damage third slot at the cost of sustainability
- **Mortefi** (Sub-DPS): Provides **Heavy Attack DMG Amplification** via Coordinated Attacks (aligned with Zani's Heavy Slash typing) and meaningful off-field Fusion damage; game8 describes Mortefi as *"a good Sub-DPS for Zani as he provides a significant Heavy Attack DMG amplification"*; the limitation noted is that Mortefi has *"no Spectro Frazzle application for Zani to cast her Heavy Slash combos"* — this is why Phoebe remains in the second slot even in this team (Spectro Rover would need to be the third slot if Mortefi is taken and Phoebe is absent)
- With Phoebe in the second slot handling Frazzle and Zani handling Heavy Slashes, Mortefi's Heavy Attack buff is a clean additive multiplier on Zani's Inferno Mode damage
- Only recommended for players comfortable without dedicated healing support

## Zani: Rotation Guide

**Standard Rotation (Phoebe team)**
Pre-rotation (Phoebe's window):
1. Phoebe (Confession mode) applies **maximum Spectro Frazzle stacks** to enemy via Confession skills; Outro Skill fires → **+100% (or 130% with sig weapon) Spectro Frazzle DMG Amplification** for Zani for 14 seconds; Frazzle stacks are at maximum when Zani enters

Zani's window (must complete primary damage within Frazzle Amplification duration):
2. Enter via **Intro Skill — Justice Descends** (→ Spectro DMG; Redundant Energy generated; converts existing enemy Frazzle stacks to Heliacal Embers on first hit)
3. Activate **Echo Skill — Capitaneus** (→ multi-hit Spectro DMG + 4 Merciless Judgements applying Frazzle; converts Frazzle to Heliacal Embers; rapid Eternal Radiance stack building)
4. **Basic Attack chain** (fills remaining Redundant Energy to 100; each hit converts any re-applied Frazzle from Phoebe's lingering effects to Heliacal Embers)
5. **Resonance Skill — Crisis Response Protocol** (hold; enters Ready Stance; releases → Spectro DMG + Heliacal Embers generated + Blaze stacks generated; Redundant Energy consumed)
6. **Resonance Liberation — Hellfire Inferno** (→ Spectro DMG; enters Inferno Mode; 8-second fatal-damage survival window active)
7. **Heavy Slash chain** (Blaze stacks consumed per slash; each consumed stack amplifies DMG multiplier; continue until Blaze depleted or Inferno Mode timer expires)
8. If Blaze is not fully depleted: additional **Crisis Response Protocol** can be used during Inferno Mode to generate more Blaze for continued Heavy Slashes
9. Outro Skill → swap to Shorekeeper or Phoebe for healing/recovery/rebuff

**Key Rotation Rules**
- Enter Inferno Mode via Liberation **after** building maximum Blaze (via Crisis Response Protocol + Frazzle → Ember conversion); casting Liberation immediately on field entry wastes Blaze-building time
- Phoebe's Outro Amplification lasts ~14 seconds — Zani must complete her Heavy Slash chain within this window; prioritize Heavy Slashes over Basic Attack fillers during Inferno Mode
- Use Crisis Response Protocol **before** Liberation to front-load Blaze; a second Crisis Protocol can be used during Inferno Mode if Redundant Energy refills during the Liberation window
- Capitaneus echo activation is best placed just before Liberation so its Frazzle application converts to Heliacal Embers on the Liberation cast's Spectro hit, boosting Eternal Radiance stacks to maximum before the Heavy Slash chain begins
- Standard Defense Protocol (block stance) during Inferno Mode still provides the parry safety window; use it aggressively in content with heavy incoming attacks during the Heavy Slash phase

**CRIT Build Target**
Target approximately **75–80% CRIT Rate** before maximizing CRIT DMG; Inherent Skill 2 provides +20% CRIT Rate passively when Frazzle is active (essentially permanent in Phoebe teams), reducing the sub-stat investment needed; verify optimal CRIT ratio with current Prydwen benchmarks.
