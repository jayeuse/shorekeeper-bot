## Phrolova Official Introduction

*"The silent and somber Overseer of the Fractsidus, Phrolova carries an endless sadness that envelops everyone around her. She weaves music that bridges the gap between life and death, crafting an ideal world within her heart."*
— Official Website (wutheringwaves.kurogames.com)

Her archive summary: *"Phrolova, a Fractsidus Overseer walking the fine line between life and death, an uncanny, deadly conductor. A silent wave of her baton is enough to attune the very frequencies of being and conduct the symphonies of 'souls.' The music of hers can sculpt a better world or, just as easily, summon a legion to wreak havoc."*

Her Drip Marketing quote: *"Do not fear the Fractsidus Overseer. Settle your mind. Quiet the clamor of thought. Only then can you stay and truly appreciate the perfect melody echoing through the Lycoris sea... and the true paradise that is soon to arrive."*

## Phrolova Personality

Phrolova's defining quality is a sadness that is not an emotion so much as a condition — described in the official introduction as *"an endless sadness that envelops everyone around her,"* and in the Fandom Backstory page as *"a pervasive sadness that seems to extend to everyone around her."* It is not performed or deployed; it is simply present, in the room, in the air, in the space between notes. People who spend time near her report feeling it without being addressed by it.

Beneath this pervasive condition is a richly philosophical and aesthetically alive inner life. The Fandom Backstory page notes that she is *"performative, inviting others to witness a 'wild side' and become complicit with her 'show'"* while also carrying *"a rich undertow of nostalgia and longing"* and occasionally showing *"a transient spark of vulnerability"* when questioning her own sanity. She considers abstract ideals — the paths of Ferris wheel houses that long to touch but never do; the difference between rebirth and mere survival — with the focused attention of someone for whom these are not philosophical exercises but personal descriptions.

She is the personification of grief organized into capability. Everything she can do — the frequency manipulation, the Tacet Discord summoning, the creation of the Lost Beyond — emerged from the same origin event (the meteor, the death, the Awakening) and has been directed toward the same goal (the return of what was lost) ever since. The fact that the goal proved unachievable did not dissolve the capability; the capability now persists without its original purpose, which is the current question about who she is.

## Phrolova Forte Examination Report

*Forte Power Designation: Symphony of Beyond / Frequency Orchestration*

**Resonance Evaluation Report**
*Evaluation Basis: [Resonance Assessment RA-F2005-G]*

*Resonator Phrolova's Awakening occurred at the time of her death, granting her the ability to tune the frequencies of her target and alter their "melodies" based on the understanding of critical frequency structuring of matters. The strength of such tuning ability is proportional to her physical condition. Latest test data indicate that under extreme Overclock conditions, while maintaining consciousness, Phrolova's tuning can be extended to a maximum of 30 times the normal range, with the longest duration record of 46 seconds.*

*Due to the complexity of its origin, the exact timing and cause of Phrolova's Awakening cannot be defined. Phrolova's Tacet Mark is located on her left arm. The Tacet Discord mutation following her Awakening prevents her body from aging, with the alteration in the color of her left iris being the sole physical change observed.*

*Test results indicate that when Phrolova's body is within a healthy state, skin reconstruction and frequency reassembly take approximately 6 seconds, while internal organ restoration requires 15–18 minutes. No fatal weakness has been found as of the writing of this evaluation.*

*Examinations reveal a large mass of chaotic frequencies housed within Phrolova's body, estimated to be around one thousand frequencies. The exact quantity remains undetermined. Further experiments confirm that these frequencies have no negative impact on her body or Resonance Ability. Such frequencies are deemed of zero significant value and are only related to Phrolova's mental fluctuations.*

*Analysis of test samples has revealed a consistent, flat Rabelle's Curve followed by a sharp ascent and stabilization. Therefore, Phrolova is identified as a **Mutant Resonator**. Through multiple experiments, it is confirmed that her method of Awakening cannot be artificially replicated upon any other individual.*

**Overclock Diagnostic Report**

*Resonator Phrolova's waveform graph displays sawtooth and needle-like fluctuations. The Time Domain pattern frequently reveals intense and chaotic activity. During testing, the Time Domain data displayed significant distortion, and the peak value remains indeterminable.*

*Resonant Criticality: Extremely low. Resonator Phrolova's frequency displays extremely low stability. It is noted that she has a documented history of Overclocking. However, she has consistently demonstrated control of her Overclocking, indicating a potential to explore methods of stabilizing and prolonging Overclock states of ordinary Resonators by leveraging this mental stability.*

*Accompanying researcher observation (marginal note):*
*Her expression was calm, yet her hands waved with fervent, exaggerated motions, sweeping across one score after another. Reason teetered, then was torn, drowned beneath emotion's storm. Notes were shattered, then reborn, twisted into sound deformed. She was the one who received their gifts. She was the one who buried their remains.*

The Forte report is structurally unusual among Wuthering Waves Forte records: it explicitly states that her Awakening *occurred at the time of her death*, which means the report is not describing how she became a Resonator but how she became something that the category of Resonator is insufficient to describe. The flat Rabelle's Curve followed by a sharp ascent is the signature of a non-standard Awakening trajectory — the incubation period ending not through biological development but through a catastrophic event that simultaneously killed her and unlocked her ability. The marginal note — the researcher's observation of her practicing alone — is the most human document in the entire archive and the only piece of the report that describes who she is rather than what she can do.

## Phrolova Character Story I: [Title Unknown — Intimacy Lv. 1]

*Full character story text not available in fetched sources at time of documentation. The story text should be retrieved directly from the in-game character interface (Intimacy Level 1) or the Wuthering Waves Fandom Wiki Backstory page and added here. The following is a summary and analysis based on contextual information from multiple secondary sources.*

**Narrative Summary**
Phrolova's first character story documents the event that defined everything: the meteor impact that destroyed Ostina, her home village, killing her family and all her childhood friends. She was a young violinist who had been away performing in a faraway city at the time; she returned to find nothing. The Forte Awakening occurred at the moment of near-death in the impact — simultaneously with the death of the world she had known. From this point forward, the story of Angel (her pre-Galbrena parallel in Septimont's narrative framework) and the story of Phrolova diverge: where Angel was struck and struck back, Phrolova was destroyed and persisted, without anything left to strike back at.

The first story is likely the account of the village, the return, and the emergence of the Resonance ability — the point where Phrolova became what she is rather than who she had been. The researcher's marginal note in the Forte report — *"She was the one who received their gifts. She was the one who buried their remains"* — may be connected to this story's events, describing her relationship to the village's legacy.

**Key thematic elements (from contextual sources):**
- The Tacet Discord mutation began at Awakening: Lycoris flowers grew across her body and left eye at the moment of emergence; TV Tropes describes this as *"body horror"* that simultaneously marks her as transformed and as belonging to the world of the dead
- The roughly one thousand frequencies she now houses are interpreted by the Villains Wiki as the absorbed frequencies of people she has lost — the literal remains of her village carried within her; the research report's statement that these frequencies *"are only related to Phrolova's mental fluctuations"* maps the village's presence to her emotional state
- The Awakening as simultaneous death and rebirth — *"occurring at the time of her death"* — establishes the philosophical framework she will spend the rest of her existence developing: death as a threshold rather than an endpoint

## Phrolova Character Story II: [Title Unknown — Intimacy Lv. 2]

*Full character story text not available in fetched sources at time of documentation. Retrieve from the in-game interface or Fandom Wiki Backstory page.*

**Narrative Summary**
The second story likely documents Phrolova's initial period after Ostina's destruction and the beginnings of her frequency manipulation abilities — how she discovered that she could preserve frequencies by absorbing them into Tacet Discords, and the first attempts to apply this to what remained of her loved ones. This is also the period that likely includes the encounter with Rover at the symphony hall built atop Ostina's ruins.

**The Symphony Hall Encounter (documented across multiple secondary sources):**
The Villains Wiki records: *"When she met Rover, she found kinship through a short but meaningful conversation following the performance of her elegy in commemoration of her people's loss at a symphony hall built atop her destroyed village. Used to people misunderstanding her work and treating her elegies with superficial artistic recognition, she felt seen by Rover's correct interpretation that her performance was an allusion to her story and grief. This led to her quickly finding a kindred spirit in Rover."*

Phrolova had been performing a composition that was not understood as a grief account — audiences received it as music, praised it as art, applied the language of aesthetic evaluation to what was in fact a memorial. Rover recognized it as what it was: the direct expression of a specific loss. This moment of recognition — being understood in the one domain where she had always been misunderstood — is the foundation of the friendship and the promise. TV Tropes notes that she calls out the Rover at the end of *Dreamcatchers in the Secret Gardens* specifically for the irony that the person who understood her grief most precisely then chose to erase their memory of her, abandoning her more completely than anyone who had merely failed to understand.

**The promise** was made by Rover in this period — they would see her again. Rover did not fulfill it. They subsequently wiped their own memory, erasing the promise along with all other bonds formed before the memory wipe.

## Phrolova Character Story III: [Title Unknown — Intimacy Lv. 3]

*Full character story text not available in fetched sources at time of documentation. Retrieve from the in-game interface or Fandom Wiki Backstory page.*

**Narrative Summary**
The third story likely documents Phrolova's path to the Fractsidus — the period of grief and abandonment after the Rover's promise went unfulfilled, and the arrival of the Fractsidus as the only entity willing to offer her what she needed. The Steam community discussion provides the clearest summary of her relationship to the organization: *"only Fractsidus offered her help, no one else did. They brought back one of her loved ones for a while, that's why she chose to work with them. They both openly acknowledge using each other. No loyalty, only benefit in their group."*

The Fractsidus did not trick Phrolova. She understood what they were and accepted their help anyway because the alternative was continuing without the only people who would help her pursue the one goal that organized her existence. The transaction was mutual and transparent. The Fandom Backstory page: *"She recognizes a relation of mutual usefulness to the Grand Architect, whom she regards as an important point of reference."*

**The Grand Architect's manipulation via Triss:**
During the events of *Dreamcatchers in the Secret Gardens*, the Grand Architect appeared to Phrolova alone and took the form of Triss — her deceased childhood friend from Ostina — to pressure her toward prioritizing Cristoforo's plans over her own goals. The Villains Wiki notes that she had previously *"liked"* him taking Triss's form (finding some comfort in the familiar presence), but when the manipulation became legible, she told him to stop. Her refusal to continue engaging with the Triss-form represents the limit of what she could be made to accept through the instrumentalization of her grief.

## Phrolova Character Story IV: [Title Unknown — Intimacy Lv. 4]

*Full character story text not available in fetched sources at time of documentation. Retrieve from the in-game interface or Fandom Wiki Backstory page.*

**Narrative Summary**
The fourth story likely covers Phrolova's active period as a Fractsidus Overseer — her role in the Carnevale sabotage attempts, her escalating engagement with the main story's events in Rinascita, and the growing exhaustion of her alliance with the Grand Architect. The TV Tropes page notes that she is a *"Wild Card"* in the narrative even after technically departing the Fractsidus — not fully aligned with either side, pursuing her own goal to the extent that any path remains open.

**The Lost Beyond's creation:**
The Villains Wiki confirms that the Lost Beyond was *"formed from the power of Leviathan"* (as revealed in dialogue between Phrolova and Cristoforo), though it was created by Phrolova specifically to recreate Ostina in a Sonoro Sphere. The sphere was imperfect — *"she couldn't maintain it, let alone perfect it"* per TV Tropes — and dissolved by the events of V2.7, forcing her return to physical reality. The Lost Beyond represents the most complete version of her original goal and its most complete failure: she built Ostina again, and it was not enough.

## Phrolova Character Story V: [Title Unknown — Intimacy Lv. 5]

*Full character story text not available in fetched sources at time of documentation. Retrieve from the in-game interface or Fandom Wiki Backstory page.*

**Narrative Summary**
The fifth story likely documents the aftermath of the main story events — Phrolova's departure from the Fractsidus, the V2.5 physical death at the hands of Rover (stabbed by Bloodpact's Pledge in the Fabricatorium of the Deep), her merger with the Lost Beyond as her frequency survived in the Sonoro Sphere she created, and the current state: the Lost Beyond having collapsed, her return to physical reality, and the open question of who she is when the goal she organized her life around can no longer be pursued in its original form.

**The death and the Sonoro Sphere:**
The Steam community post summarizing the item received after the V2.5 quest: *"it is Phrolova's remaining forte, it says how the blade pierced through her chest, her tears and blood became the sonoro."* The item confirms the physical body perished. Game8's confirmed update notes that conversations with Abby (Abraxas) beside the mirror confirm she is alive within the Lost Beyond — *"the Lost Beyond pulses together with the frequency of Phrolova."* This is the version of her that players can visit in the field after completing *Dreamcatchers in the Secret Gardens*.

The TV Tropes page describes her current state as *"considering a new path for herself, but we aren't sure what that means, and she probably doesn't know yet either."* The fifth story is likely the most direct available statement of what she does with the open question her life has become.

## Phrolova: Voice Lines

**Thoughts: I**
*"I control the phrasing and dynamic balance through the rise and fall of my hands, shaping the notes into the sounds I desire. Through waves of pain and pleasure, I drive the orchestra toward its climax, where no one falls behind and none can escape. Until... until the symphony in my heart finds its perfect form."*
The most complete available statement of her inner aesthetic and psychological drive: the conducting metaphor applied not to music but to reality itself; the goal (the perfect form of the symphony in her heart) as the unreachable point she perpetually moves toward. The phrase *"where no one falls behind and none can escape"* is simultaneously a description of her ideal orchestral performance and of the absorbing, encompassing nature of the Forte power she wields over frequencies.

**Thoughts: II**
*(Referenced in the Hobby voice line section — the redcurrant flavor preference)*
*"The taste of redcurrants... the only thing that has left an impression on me. Its sour and bitter taste, perhaps? I find it quite tolerable actually. Maybe I've simply grown used to it..."*
The only food preference voice line in which she acknowledges having grown accustomed to something difficult rather than pleasant; consistent with her broader relationship to everything that is hard: she tolerates it, lives with it, finds it familiar.

**Chat: I**
*"Hecate has always stood at the crossroads of the other world, waiting, as if knowing that our paths would inevitably meet. Only when I placed my hand in its palm did I realize — Hecate is me. The me of the other world, the me of this world, and the me who moves freely between them... From that moment on, 'I' became the guardian of the boundary, perceiving all, seeing even the farthest reaches of both worlds."*
The foundational statement of the Hecate identification. The crossroads is Hecate's mythological domain (goddess of crossroads); the waiting is the Tacet Discord waiting at the convergence of decaying frequencies in the Beyond; the meeting of palms is the moment of integration. The self-identification is not metaphorical. She is now — in her own understanding — the guardian of the boundary between the world of the living and the world of the dead, perceiving both, belonging fully to neither.

**Chat: II**
*"Overclocking is already common practice for the Fractsidus. I've seen countless people walk toward it, seize control, and exploit it. Of course, they are fully aware of the dangers it brings, death included. Don't disagree just yet. If you look at it from another angle, you'll see it offers not just a hopeless path to death, but a chance for a different kind of rebirth. After all, humans are creatures who seek safety, threatened by fear, clinging to life at all costs. But we... we should master not only living, but living even after death."*
Phrolova's philosophy of death, compressed into a single voice line. The pronoun shift at the end — from *"humans are creatures who"* to *"we should master"* — is the tell: she is no longer speaking as a human who has thought about death philosophically, but as someone who has been through it and emerged with a different relationship to it. *"We"* here does not mean all people; it means people like her.

**Trouble Voice Line**
*"After enough failures, the sting fades, and with it, the burden of worry."*
The most concisely devastating voice line available. This is either wisdom or exhaustion; it is probably both. She has failed to resurrect her village enough times, in enough forms, that the specific grief of each failure has been absorbed into the general condition of carrying the grief. The sting of individual failures fades because the larger context already hurts so much that nothing can reliably sting anymore. Whether this is equanimity or numbness is the same question her entire arc is asking.

**Ideals**
*"I wish to comprehend the cycle of life by experiencing it over and over again."*
Phrolova does not have a conventional ideals statement about a better world or a protected person or a just outcome. She has an epistemological aspiration: to understand something. The thing she wants to understand is the cycle of life — and the method is first-person repetitive experience. She has already died once. She has already been through the Overclocking that others call the threshold of death. She is currently in a state somewhere between the living and the dead. The ideal is to eventually understand, through accumulated experience, what all of this means. The aspiration is humble for someone of her capability, and enormous for someone of her situation.

**Favorite Food**
*"The taste of redcurrants... the only thing that has left an impression on me. Its sour and bitter taste, perhaps? I find it quite tolerable actually. Maybe I've simply grown used to it..."*
The favorite food is something she tolerates and has grown used to, not something she actively enjoys. The sour and bitter taste is described as the impression rather than the pleasure. For a character who has been grieving her village for what may be centuries, this is a characteristically honest relationship to preference: she does not pretend to find things sweet that are not.

**Disliked Food**
*"After meat is smoked or roasted, its taste is similar to... Never mind."*
The trailing off is one of the most noted voice lines among players, interpreted as the meat's smell or taste recalling something — the fires, the ruins, the aftermath of the meteor; the ashes of Ostina. She does not name it. The *"Never mind"* is not evasion; it is a decision not to say the thing out loud to Rover.

## Phrolova: Narrative Chronicle

- **[Ostina — Birth and Childhood]** Phrolova is born in the ancient village of Ostina, in an unknown nation on Solaris-3; she grows up as a violinist, trained from childhood; her family and all childhood friends live in the village; she is a person of musical talent and developing philosophical depth
- **[The Meteor — The End of Ostina]** A catastrophic meteor shower destroys Ostina entirely; all residents are killed; Phrolova was away performing in a faraway city at the time; she returns to find nothing; at the moment of near-death in the impact's aftermath, her Forte awakens — simultaneously with her death; Lycoris flowers grow across her body and left eye; the Tacet Discord mutation renders her ageless from this point forward
- **[The Awakening's Aftermath]** Phrolova discovers she can manipulate and transfigure frequencies; she begins exploring whether this ability can be used to preserve or eventually restore the people she lost; she absorbs approximately one thousand frequencies into her body — the remains of Ostina's residents stored within her; she carries these frequencies as the primary organizing fact of her existence
- **[Meeting Rover — The Promise]** At a symphony hall built atop Ostina's ruins, Phrolova performs an elegy for her village; Rover attends and recognizes the performance as a grief account rather than a musical composition — the only person to have done so; a friendship forms; Rover makes a promise to see her again; Rover subsequently does not return and wipes their own memory, erasing the promise and all bonds formed before the memory wipe; this abandonment is the direct cause of Phrolova's Fractsidus affiliation
- **[The Fractsidus — Accepting the Only Available Help]** The Fractsidus is the only entity that offers to help Phrolova pursue her goal; they temporarily resurrect one of her loved ones through their methods; she accepts their help on explicitly mutual terms — no loyalty, only benefit; she rises to the rank of Overseer; the Grand Architect uses the form of Triss (her deceased childhood friend) to manipulate her during joint operations, something she tolerates for a time before refusing
- **[Active Overseer Period — V1.0 to V2.5]** Phrolova appears as the most persistent antagonist figure in the main story from the very beginning of the game through the end of the Rinascita arc; she attempts to sabotage the Carnevale; she commands Hecate and other Tacet Discords; she creates the Lost Beyond (a Sonoro Sphere powered by Leviathan's frequency that recreates Ostina)
- **[V2.5 — Physical Death in the Fabricatorium of the Deep]** During the final confrontation over the Gem, Rover throws Bloodpact's Pledge; the blade pierces Phrolova's chest; her tears and blood became the Sonoro as she falls into the Fabricatorium's depths; her physical body is destroyed; her frequency merges with the Lost Beyond, which continues to exist as her Sonoro Sphere; Abby (Abraxas) confirms she is alive within it
- **[The Lost Beyond — Phrolova's Current Status]** Players who complete *Dreamcatchers in the Secret Gardens* can visit the Lost Beyond and interact with Phrolova; the Lost Beyond pulses with her frequency; she has re-created Ostina within it; she is surrounded by the frequencies she has carried; she is with Hecate; the dialogue with Rover in the Lost Beyond is the closest the main story comes to a genuine conversation between them as equals rather than as adversaries
- **[V2.7 — Physical Return]** The Lost Beyond dissolves — it was imperfect and could not be maintained indefinitely; Phrolova returns to physical reality, reportedly due to her TD mutation's regenerative ability; she appears in *Dawn Breaks on Dark Tides* commanding Tacet Discords alongside Scar and Cristoforo in a final Fractsidus operation; she departs the Fractsidus at the end of Act XI after completing the Grand Architect's final task; Schwarzloch permits the departure, viewing convergence of their goals as inevitable regardless
- **[Current State — V2.7 and Beyond]** Phrolova is no longer affiliated with the Fractsidus; she is not allied with Rover's faction; she is, per TV Tropes, *"a Wild Card"*; she is *"considering a new path, but we aren't sure what that means, and she probably doesn't know yet either"*; the Lost Beyond has dissolved; the goal of resurrecting Ostina has failed; she is alive, ageless, and without the organizing purpose of her existence for the first time since the meteor

## Phrolova: Thematic Notes and Cultural Allusions

**The Lycoris (Red Spider Lily)**
The lycoris — higanbana in Japanese, 彼岸花 (*higan-bana*, "the other shore flower") — is the flower associated in Japanese and Chinese tradition with the transition between the living world and the afterlife. It grows at the boundary between the two. The Fandom Wiki notes this association explicitly in its character page: *"The lycoris or red spider lily is a flower that symbolizes death and the afterlife, especially in Japan and China."* Phrolova wields it as a conductor's baton, wears the color vocabulary of its red and white in her outfit, and lives in a place called the Lycoris sea. She is the flower.

**Hecate (Greek Mythology)**
Hecate in Greek mythology is the goddess of witchcraft, magic, ghosts, necromancy, and crossroads — specifically the three-way crossroads that represent places of decision and transition between worlds. She is a three-faced deity in her full representation, capable of seeing in all directions simultaneously and guarding the thresholds between realms. The WuWa Hecate is a three-faced Tacet Discord from a Sonoro Sphere where all decaying frequencies converge, described as a *"Witch of the Underworld."* The crossroads alignment is made explicit by the Fandom Wiki: *"Hecate is a goddess from Greek mythology associated with magic, witchcraft, and fittingly, with crossroads, similar to Phrolova's association with the Lost Beyond."*

**"Phrolova" — Name Etymology**
Derived from the Russian *Frol* (a given name variant meaning "wise man," from the Latin *Florus*, meaning "flowering") and the Slavic feminine patronymic suffix *-ova* ("belonging to" or "of the family of"). The name positions Phrolova as belonging to a tradition of wisdom or discernment — consistent with her role as someone who has accumulated the frequencies of a thousand individuals and retains genuine insight into the nature of death and existence. The name also connects her to Scar's Russian-adjacent naming scheme and to the broader Eastern European cultural register of several Fractsidus members.

**The Symphony as Total Domination**
The Fandom Backstory page's description of Phrolova's secret obsession — *"driving an orchestra to a peak where no one falls behind and none can escape"* — mirrors the description in her Thoughts I voice line exactly: *"where no one falls behind and none can escape."* This phrase is simultaneously the conductor's aesthetic ideal (perfect collective execution, total unity of purpose) and the frequency manipulator's operational description (everyone absorbed, no one exempt). The double meaning is not accidental.
