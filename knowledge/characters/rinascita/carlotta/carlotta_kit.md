## Carlotta: Combat Archetype and Role

- **Element/Attribute:** Glacio
- **Weapon Type:** Pistols
- **Role:** Main DPS — Resonance Skill and Liberation-focused single-target DPS. Carlotta deals the overwhelming majority of her damage through Resonance Skill hits (Art of Violence, Chromatic Splendor) and Liberation Skill DMG (Death Knell, Fatal Finale). Her kit is designed around two interlocking resources — Moldable Crystals and Substance — that feed into each other to produce a high-burst, repeatable damage loop. She is the game's first limited 5-star Pistol character and the first prominent Glacio hypercarry built for single-target priority damage.
- **Tier:** T0 at launch (Version 2.0); T1 in subsequent metas as power creep elevated newer characters, but remains competitive and is considered one of the cleanest and most satisfying DPS designs in the game

## Carlotta: Key Resources (Forte Mechanics Overview)

Carlotta's combat system centers on two tracked resources:

**Moldable Crystals (0–6 maximum):**
Small crystalline shards generated by casting Intro Skill, Resonance Skill, and Heavy Attack. Crystals are consumed by Necessary Measures (the enhanced Basic Attack), by Dodge Counter, and by Chromatic Splendor (the Resonance Skill follow-up). Each Moldable Crystal consumed refills Substance. Crystals cannot exceed 6; any generation past maximum is lost, so timing is important.

**Substance (Forte Gauge, 0–120; divided into 2 segments):**
Filled by consuming Moldable Crystals and by Resonance Skill actions. When Substance is full, Heavy Attack transforms from a standard charged strike into **Containment Tactics** (standard Forte Heavy) or **Heavy Attack Imminent Oblivion** (unlocked by Inherent Passives) — consuming all Substance for massive Glacio DMG and reducing the Resonance Skill cooldown by 6s. The diamond-shaped **Tinted Crystal** indicator in the middle of the Forte gauge turns red when Imminent Oblivion conditions are met (full Substance + active Liberation Resonance Skill bonus), signaling the window for the highest-multiplier strike in her kit.

**Liberation Mode — Twilight Tango (10s duration):**
Active during Resonance Liberation. While in Twilight Tango, Normal Attack or the Liberation button casts **Death Knell** — a musket shot + 4 crystal shards (all classified as Resonance Skill DMG), each granting 1 **Meta Vector**. At 4 Meta Vectors, the next input casts **Fatal Finale** — AoE Glacio DMG (Resonance Skill class) that ends Twilight Tango. Targets hit by the Liberation's initial blast are inflicted with **Deconstruction**, which causes all subsequent DMG against them to ignore **18% of their DEF**, making the Liberation window a DEF-shred amplifier for everything she fires during it.

## Carlotta Forte Circuit: Vegetative Universe

**Moldable Crystal generation sources:**
- Intro Skill Wintertime Aria: grants Moldable Crystals on cast
- Resonance Skill Art of Violence (first press): grants Moldable Crystals
- Heavy Attack: grants Moldable Crystals
- Containment Tactics / Imminent Oblivion: the Forte Heavy attacks grant Moldable Crystals on use

**Substance generation sources:**
- Each Moldable Crystal consumed by Necessary Measures or Dodge Counter → fills Substance
- Resonance Skill and its follow-ups → fill Substance
- Intro Skill → fills Substance

**Imminent Oblivion condition:** Inherent Passive 2 (Opal Night) unlocks the special Heavy Attack Imminent Oblivion, which deals substantially higher damage than Containment Tactics and also reduces Resonance Skill cooldown. The Tinted Crystal indicator goes red when Substance is full and the Resonance Skill DMG Bonus buff (from a Liberation or Intro cast) is active, indicating the maximum-value Heavy Attack window.

**Rotation principle:** Carlotta's rotation is designed around a 14s Resonance Skill cooldown that the Forte Heavy reduces by 6s (effectively giving her a ~8s functional cooldown), enabling her to cast the Skill approximately twice per rotation, building toward Liberation. The Skill is her primary damage source and the Forte Heavy is her reset mechanism and secondary damage spike.

## Carlotta: Character Kit: Basic Attack — Silent Execution

**Standard chain (no Moldable Crystals):** Carlotta performs 2 consecutive strikes dealing Glacio DMG. This is the fallback chain when Moldable Crystals are absent.

**Basic Attack Necessary Measures (with Moldable Crystals):** Carlotta's Basic Attack chain becomes an enhanced 3-hit combo, each hit consuming 1 Moldable Crystal and dealing Glacio DMG. Each consumed crystal contributes Substance generation, making Necessary Measures both the primary offensive Basic chain and the primary Substance-building loop. With 3 crystals available, a full Stage 1 → Stage 2 → Stage 3 chain consumes them all.

**Heavy Attack:** Standard charged strike costing STA, dealing Glacio DMG.

**Heavy Attack Containment Tactics (Substance full):** Replaces Heavy Attack. Consumes all Substance to deal Glacio DMG and reduce Resonance Skill Art of Violence cooldown by 6s. Generates 15 Concerto.

**Mid-air Attack / Customary Greetings:** Plunging attack (STA cost). Following up with Basic Attack immediately after landing triggers Customary Greetings: Carlotta flips over the target and fires a surprise shot, dealing Glacio DMG.

**Dodge Counter:** After a successful dodge, Normal Attack casts a riposte shot dealing Glacio DMG. Consumes 1 Moldable Crystal.

## Carlotta: Character Kit: Resonance Skill — Art of Violence

**First press — Art of Violence:** Carlotta fires a Glacio attack inflicting **Dispersion** on the target — immobilizing them for 1.5s. Grants Moldable Crystals. Must follow up with a second Skill press to activate Chromatic Splendor, or the Skill enters cooldown (14s).

**Second press — Chromatic Splendor:** Follow-up cast consuming all Moldable Crystals. Deals Glacio DMG in a 3-hit sequence (2 hits + 1 large burst). Classified as Resonance Skill DMG. The crystal consumption fills Substance. Cooldown: 14s (reduced to ~8s effective with Containment Tactics/Imminent Oblivion).

This 2-press structure means every Skill activation is effectively a Skill→Skill combo. Chromatic Splendor is where the majority of the Resonance Skill damage lives: the final hit (170.10% at Lv.1, scaling to 338.18% at Lv.10) is a significant single hit that benefits from every Resonance Skill DMG bonus stack active.

## Carlotta: Character Kit: Resonance Liberation — Era of New Wave

Carlotta fires her large crystal musket, dealing AoE Glacio DMG (classified as Resonance Skill DMG) to all enemies in the area and inflicting **Deconstruction** on hit targets. Deconstruction causes all subsequent DMG against those targets to **ignore 18% of their DEF** for its duration. After casting, Carlotta enters **Twilight Tango** (10s).

**Twilight Tango — Death Knell:** Each press of Normal Attack or Liberation button fires one Death Knell — a musket shot + 4 crystal shards. Each Death Knell deals Glacio DMG (Resonance Skill class) and grants 1 Meta Vector. Carlotta can move laterally with each shot.

**Fatal Finale:** At 4 Meta Vectors, Normal Attack or Liberation fires Fatal Finale — dealing AoE Glacio DMG (Resonance Skill class), ending Twilight Tango.

All standard Substance actions are suspended during Twilight Tango (no Necessary Measures, Containment Tactics, or Imminent Oblivion). The Liberation sequence is: cast → 4× Death Knell → Fatal Finale = 5 significant damage instances in approximately 6–8 seconds.

**Important note:** The Liberation initial cast plus the Death Knell and Fatal Finale sequences are ALL classified as Resonance Skill DMG. This means Resonance Skill DMG Bonus from Zhezhi's Outro, from The Last Dance weapon, and from the Frosty Resolve echo set all apply to the Liberation sequence as well. This is the design linchpin of Carlotta's kit: she effectively has a Resonance Skill character whose Liberation is *also* Resonance Skill class, allowing one damage type buff to amplify essentially her entire output.

## Carlotta: Inherent Passives

**Crystal Aesthetics (Inherent 1):** Carlotta's Resonance Skill DMG Bonus increases by **10%** for each Moldable Crystal consumed by Chromatic Splendor. The cap is 3 Crystals per Chromatic Splendor activation (3 × 10% = **+30% Resonance Skill DMG Bonus** from this passive alone). This bonus is the reason maximizing crystal count before Chromatic Splendor is a key optimization target.

**Opal Night (Inherent 2):** After using Intro Skill or casting Resonance Liberation, Carlotta's Heavy Attack is upgraded to **Heavy Attack Imminent Oblivion** for a limited window (10s after Intro or Liberation). Imminent Oblivion deals significantly higher damage than Containment Tactics (approximately 800%+ total Glacio DMG at high levels), still reduces Resonance Skill cooldown by 6s, and also applies **Imminent Oblivion's DEF Penetration buff** to targets. The Tinted Crystal indicator turns red to signal the active window.

This passive is what gives Carlotta a distinctive rhythm: every rotation should begin with an Intro Skill (triggering the Imminent Oblivion window), then proceed through Skills and Forte Heavy within that 10s enhancement period, before using Liberation to open a second window within the same rotation.

## Carlotta: Intro/Outro Skills

**Intro Skill — Wintertime Aria:**
Carlotta leaps in, attacking the target and dealing Glacio DMG in a 3-hit sequence (1 main hit + 2 follow-up shots). Grants Moldable Crystals. Fills Substance. Generates 10 Concerto. Triggers the Inherent 2 Imminent Oblivion enhancement window.

**Outro Skill — Closing Remark:**
Carlotta deals Glacio DMG equal to **329.24% of ATK** in a finishing strike, then exits the field. This is a straightforward outro; its enhancement at S1 adds an additional **Kaleidoscope Sparks** strike at the end dealing Glacio DMG equal to **1032.18% of ATK**, making the S1 Outro one of the strongest swap-damage hits in the game when combined with active buffs.

## Carlotta: Resonance Chains (Sequences)

**S1 — Sequence Node 1:**
Enables **Outro Skill Kaleidoscope Sparks**: the Outro now includes 1 additional strike dealing Glacio DMG equal to **1032.18% of Carlotta's ATK**. The DMG Multiplier of Resonance Skill Art of Violence and Chromatic Splendor is increased by **93%**. S1 is one of the highest-value first sequences in the game: the 93% multiplier on both Skill hits is roughly a 40–50% personal DPS increase from the Skill alone, and the Outro amplification means Carlotta's swap-out also deals significant damage for the first time.

**S2 — Sequence Node 2:**
Casting Heavy Attack, Containment Tactics, or Imminent Oblivion grants all team members **+25% Resonance Skill DMG Bonus for 30s**. This is a team buff that activates every time Carlotta triggers her Forte Heavy (which is once per rotation minimum) — it benefits herself during the Liberation window and her teammates while they're on field. Meaningfully improves team DPS while also adding to Carlotta's own Liberation output.

**S3 — Sequence Node 3:**
The DMG Multiplier of **Heavy Attack Imminent Oblivion is increased by +47%**. A direct amplification of the Forte rotation's signature strike. Straightforward damage increase; less impactful than S1 but still a noticeable boost to the most visually impressive hit in her kit.

**S4 — Sequence Node 4:**
Death Knell shots (from Liberation Twilight Tango) deal higher damage and fire **double the number of crystal shards** — representing a total **+186.6% DMG Multiplier** increase to each Death Knell hit. Additionally, Death Knell inflicts **Scattering** on targets, immobilizing them for 1.5s (removed after 1.5s or after receiving DMG). The Death Knell doubling converts the Liberation from a primarily burst-focused sequence into a sustained high-damage stream during Twilight Tango, and the intermittent immobilization is a meaningful combat quality-of-life upgrade.

## Carlotta: Recommended Echo Sets

**Best Build — Frosty Resolve (5-piece):**
Carlotta's definitive set, introduced in Version 2.0 as part of Rinascita's echo pool. The 5-piece bonus stacks two complementary effects: casting Resonance Skill grants **+22.5% Glacio DMG Bonus** (up to 2 stacks = +45% Glacio DMG Bonus at full stacks) and casting Resonance Liberation grants **+18% Resonance Skill DMG Bonus** per stack (up to 2 = +36% Resonance Skill DMG Bonus). Both effects are trivially activated by Carlotta's normal rotation. Combined, the full set provides: +45% Glacio DMG + up to +36% Resonance Skill DMG — directly amplifying both her primary damage type and her primary damage classification simultaneously.

**Main Echo — Sentry Construct (4-cost):** The dedicated main echo for Carlotta. Activating the Sentry Construct transforms into the Sentry and attacks enemies dealing **405% Glacio DMG**. Each Liberation cast charges the Strike Capacitor; at full charge, the next Resonance Skill cast fires an empowered strike. It carries the Frosty Resolve sonata effect and is designed to synergize directly with Carlotta's Liberation → Skill → Skill rotation loop. **Best-in-slot main echo with no practical competitor** at current patch levels.

**Alternative Set — Freezing Frost (5-piece):** Grants **+40% Glacio DMG Bonus** at 5 stacks. Weaker than Frosty Resolve for Carlotta because it provides no Resonance Skill DMG scaling, but viable as a placeholder during early farming or for players transitioning from pre-2.0 Glacio builds. Pair with Lampylumen Myriad as the 4-cost echo when using this set.

**Cost Pattern:** ④ ③ ③ ① ①

**Stat Priority:**
- 4-cost: Crit Rate or Crit DMG (whichever balances the ratio toward 1:2)
- 3-cost: ATK% or Glacio DMG Bonus (ATK% preferred unless substat ATK% is already very high)
- 1-cost: ATK% × 2
- Substats: Crit Rate ★★★ > Crit DMG ★★★ > ATK% ★★ > Energy Regen ★★ (until ~120–125%) > Resonance Skill DMG ★

**Energy Regen target:** 120–125% ER. Unlike supports, Carlotta wants just enough ER to cast Liberation every rotation. Below ~120% risks delayed Liberation and broken rotations; above 125% wastes substat budget that could be Crit or ATK.

## Carlotta: Best Weapon

**The Last Dance (Signature, 5-star Pistol):**
ATK +12%. On Intro Skill or Liberation cast: Resonance Skill DMG Bonus increases by **+96% for 5s**. This weapon is purpose-built for Carlotta's rotation loop: every rotation starts with an Intro (triggering the buff) and includes a Liberation (triggering it again), ensuring nearly permanent uptime on the +96% Resonance Skill DMG Bonus. Since Carlotta's Liberation hits are classified as Resonance Skill DMG, the Liberation window itself benefits from the buff it just triggered. Combined with Frosty Resolve's stacking and Chromatic Splendor's Crystal passive, Carlotta can reach enormous Resonance Skill DMG Bonus values during her burst window.

**Phasic Homogenizer (5-star alternative):** As of Version 2.6 community reports, Phasic Homogenizer is noted to outperform The Last Dance in certain high-investment configurations. Players should verify against current patch DPS calculations before assuming signature is best-in-slot.

**Emerald of Genesis (5-star alternative):** Energy Regen + ATK. Good for players who cannot reach 120% ER through echoes alone.

**Static Mist (4-star):** ATK% stat with Energy Regen substat. Best accessible 4-star option for reaching ER target while maintaining attack scaling.

**Thunderbolt / Undying Flame (4-star alternatives):** Resonance Skill DMG bonus passives. Directly synergize with Carlotta's damage type but require careful ER management without Static Mist's ER contribution.

**Guardian Pistols (F2P 4-star):** ATK% + +12% Resonance Skill DMG Bonus. Solid free-to-play option with direct Resonance Skill synergy; best F2P choice until a 4-star alternative is available.

## Carlotta: Best Teams

**Team 1 — Premium (BIS):**
**Carlotta** (Main DPS) / Zhezhi (Sub-DPS / Glacio Buffer) / Shorekeeper (Healer / Crit Buffer).

Zhezhi's Outro grants Carlotta **Glacio DMG Bonus + Resonance Skill DMG Bonus** — the two stats Carlotta scales most heavily with. Shorekeeper provides up to +12.5% Crit Rate, +25% Crit DMG, +15% Damage Amplification, and continuous healing. This trio is considered one of the strongest team compositions in Version 2.0 and remains competitive in subsequent patches, as all three characters' value scales together. Full rotation: Shorekeeper Liberation → Outro → Zhezhi Intro → Zhezhi optimal combo → Zhezhi Liberation → Outro (Glacio + Skill DMG buff applied) → Carlotta Intro (Imminent Oblivion window) → Skill → Imminent Oblivion → Skill (+ Liberation mid-rotation) → Death Knell ×4 → Fatal Finale → swap.

**Team 2 — Standard (Verina variant):**
**Carlotta** (Main DPS) / Zhezhi (Sub-DPS / Buffer) / Verina (Healer / ATK Buffer).

Verina provides ATK% buff and off-field coordinated attacks + healing. Lower Crit buff ceiling than Shorekeeper but higher accessibility. The Zhezhi Outro buff still fully applies, and Verina's healing is more forgiving for players learning Carlotta's rotation.

**Team 3 — Jinhsi Variant (Coordinated Attack):**
**Carlotta** (Main DPS) / Jinhsi (Sub-DPS) / Shorekeeper (Healer / Crit Buffer).

Jinhsi's coordinated attack mechanics can supplement Carlotta's single-target burst between Skill cooldowns. Less synergistic than the Zhezhi pairing (Jinhsi brings Spectro rather than Glacio/Skill DMG) but works as an alternative if Zhezhi is unavailable.

**Team 4 — Budget:**
**Carlotta** (Main DPS) / Sanhua (Sub-DPS / Basic ATK buffer) / Verina or Baizhi (Healer).

Sanhua's Outro provides Basic Attack DMG Amplification, which partially applies to Carlotta's Necessary Measures and standard attack chain but does not amplify her primary Resonance Skill damage. Weaker than Zhezhi but available to all players. Sanhua's extremely short field time preserves Carlotta's rotation timing.
