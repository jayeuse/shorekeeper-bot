# Ciaccona Kit Documentation

## Ciaccona: Combat Archetype and Role
- **Element/Attribute:** Aero
- **Weapon Type:** Pistols
- **Role:** Sub-DPS / Aero Erosion Enabler — applies Aero Erosion (and optionally Spectro Frazzle) rapidly via Basic Attack, Skill, Intro, and Resonance Liberation; buffs team Aero DMG by +24% through persistent off-field Ensemble Sylphs performing Solo Concert; delivers +100% Aero Erosion DMG Amplification via Outro; deals significant off-field Resonance Liberation DMG during Recital that persists across multiple waves of enemies even when not on field
- **Scaling:** ATK (standard ATK-based; CRIT DMG is the Forte node sub-stat, making CRIT DMG stacking and CRIT Rate investment the primary build goal)
- **Damage Profile:** Heavy Attack DMG (Quadruple Downbeat), Aero DMG (Resonance Liberation — Singer's Triple Cadenza, Tonic waves), Basic Attack DMG (BA chain); her off-field Tonic DMG during Recital constitutes meaningful passive damage across sustained encounters and multi-wave content
- **Best-fit Teams:** Cartethyia + Ciaccona + Chisa/Aero Rover (S-Tier Aero Erosion); Phoebe + Ciaccona + Support (Spectro Frazzle); any Aero team with sustained Erosion requirements; viable as Main DPS in AoE-heavy content
- **Key flexibility advantage:** Can toggle her Liberation between Aero Erosion mode (green Tonics) and Spectro Frazzle mode (yellow Tonics), making her usable across both major negative-status team archetypes

## Ciaccona: Key Resources (Forte Mechanics Overview)

Ciaccona's kit is built around three interconnected systems: the **Ensemble Sylph** (a wind clone of herself generated through specific interactions), the **Musical Essence** Forte gauge (0–3 segments), and the **Recital** state from her Resonance Liberation.

**Ensemble Sylphs** are the permanent support layer: once two are active, they continuously perform Solo Concert, providing the team +24% Aero DMG Bonus with essentially 100% uptime as long as Ciaccona cycles BA4 correctly. They are generated by cancelling BA Stage 4 or Solo Concert with a jump or skill, and they complete any attack animation Ciaccona interrupts — meaning there is no damage loss from cancelling.

**Musical Essence** (3 segments max) is generated by BA Stage 4 or Intro Skill (1 segment each). At 3 segments, Heavy Attack upgrades to **Quadruple Downbeat** — her highest-DPS Forte hit, which also pulls in enemies and applies Aero Erosion, and generates 25 Concerto Energy to close out her rotation.

**Recital** is the off-field component: activated by Liberation, it persists even when Ciaccona is switched out. While in Recital, sound waves radiate periodically and can be triggered (manually or auto, depending on whether she's on or off field) for Tonic waves that apply either Aero Erosion or Spectro Frazzle to all nearby enemies — indefinitely, across wave transitions.

## Ciaccona Forte Circuit: Musical Essence / Ensemble Sylph

**Ensemble Sylph generation triggers:**
- BA Stage 4 interrupted by Jump → Sylph generated; BA4 animation completed by Sylph anyway
- BA Stage 4 interrupted by Resonance Skill — Harmonic Allegro → Sylph generated (Sylph does NOT enter Solo Concert in this case)
- Solo Concert interrupted by Jump or enemy hit → Sylph generated; Solo Concert continues via Sylph
- Resonance Skill interrupting BA, Heavy, Mid-air, or Solo Concert → Sylph generated

**Ensemble Sylph rules:**
- Maximum 2 Sylphs simultaneously; the oldest is removed when a 3rd would be created
- Sylphs performing Solo Concert grant **+24% Aero DMG Bonus** to all nearby Resonators (unstackable — 2 Sylphs does not grant 48%)
- During Recital: Sylphs can directly grant the Solo Concert Aero DMG Bonus even while Ciaccona is off-field

**Musical Essence (0–3 segments):**
- +1 segment from: Basic Attack Stage 4 (with or without interruption), Intro Skill — Roaming with the Wind
- At 3 segments: Heavy Attack → **Heavy Attack — Quadruple Downbeat**
  - Shoots Downbeat Notes forward, pulling nearby targets in
  - Deals Aero DMG (15.80%×10 + 157.95% at Lv. 1)
  - Inflicts **1 stack of Aero Erosion** on targets hit
  - Generates **25 Concerto Energy** — the primary Concerto finisher in her rotation
  - Can be cast mid-air close to ground
  - Inherent Passive — Winds of Rinascita: Quadruple Downbeat DMG +30%

**Optimal BA4 cycle for Main DPS:**
Mid-air Attack Stage 2 → BA Stage 4 (BA4 → Jump cancel → Mid-air 1 → Mid-air 2 → BA4 → Jump cancel → repeat) chains BA4s rapidly, building Musical Essence stacks faster and sustaining Ensemble Sylph presence continuously.

## Ciaccona: Character Kit: Basic Attack — Quadruple Time Steps

**Basic Attack (4-hit chain; all Aero DMG):**
- Stage 1: 28.70%
- Stage 2: 24.60% + 12.30%×2 + 32.80%
- Stage 3: 16.61%×4
- Stage 4: 30.75%×4 — **inflicts 1 Aero Erosion; triggers Solo Concert; generates Ensemble Sylph if interrupted by Jump**

*Stages 1–2 are skipped entirely in optimized play (Intro Skill → BA Stage 3 → BA Stage 4 is the standard entry)*

**Heavy Attack** (baseline; 25 STA): 54.12% Aero DMG; jumps into mid-air to attack
**Aimed Attack** (Aiming Mode): 16.40% (shot) / 36.90% (fully charged); Aero DMG; Heavy Attack DMG tag
**Mid-air Attack Stage 1:** 27.88%×2; STA 15
**Mid-air Attack Stage 2:** 12.30%×4; STA 15 — pressing Normal Attack after Stage 2 chains into **Basic Attack Stage 4**
**Dodge Counter:** 28.76%×4; Aero DMG

## Ciaccona: Character Kit: Resonance Skill — Harmonic Allegro

Ciaccona moves a short distance (dodge-like repositioning), dealing **20.32%×4 Aero DMG** and inflicting **1 stack of Aero Erosion** on the target hit.
- Cooldown: 10s; Concerto Regen: 15
- Can be cast mid-air
- Press Normal Attack after Skill to chain into Basic Attack Stage 2
- **Generates an Ensemble Sylph** when it interrupts BA, Heavy Attack, Mid-air Attack, or Solo Concert (but this Sylph does NOT enter Solo Concert — use Jump-cancel BA4 instead for Sylph Solo Concert generation)

**Practical rotation note:** Harmonic Allegro is primarily used for its Aero Erosion application, Concerto Regen (15 is the highest single non-Liberation source), and repositioning utility rather than for Sylph generation. Its interrupt-Sylph doesn't contribute to the Solo Concert buff, so the skill is slotted in rotations after Quadruple Downbeat to accelerate the Liberation cooldown.

## Ciaccona: Character Kit: Resonance Liberation — Singer's Triple Cadenza

Ciaccona and her Ensemble Sylphs perform an **Improvised Symphonic Poem**, dealing **553.50% Aero DMG** once to all nearby targets, then entering **Recital**.
- Cooldown: 20s; Resonance Energy Cost: 125; Concerto Regen: 20
- Each successful Tonic interaction during Recital: +10 Concerto Regen

**Recital state (persistent off-field):**
- Sound waves radiate periodically (approx. every 1.6s); player can press green or yellow button when wave overlaps the circle indicator
- **Green Tonic:** Deals Aero DMG to nearby targets + inflicts **1 Aero Erosion** per wave (3.08%×20 Aero DMG total across Recital)
- **Yellow Tonic:** Deals Aero DMG to nearby targets + inflicts **1 Spectro Frazzle** per wave
- Switching to another character during Recital: Recital does NOT end; instead, Tonics auto-generate matching the last manually selected color (green default if no input was made before swap)
- Ciaccona can be switched back onto the field to resume manual Tonic input
- Ensemble Sylphs directly grant Solo Concert Aero DMG Bonus to the team during Recital

**Defensive Recital properties:**
- Ciaccona takes **50% less DMG** during Recital
- Ciaccona is **immune to interruptions** during Recital
- Inherent Skill — Interlude Tune: Casting Liberation grants a Shield equal to **100% of Max HP** for 4s (removed on character swap)

**Tonic color toggling:**
The Liberation defaults to Aero Erosion (green) mode. To switch to Spectro Frazzle (yellow) mode, the player must quickly press yellow after the Liberation activates before swapping out — the last color selected before swap is the auto-trigger color throughout the rest of Recital. This allows deliberate team composition flexibility between Erosion and Frazzle teams without rebuilding.

## Ciaccona: Inherent Passives

**Interlude Tune**
- Casting Resonance Liberation — Singer's Triple Cadenza grants Ciaccona a Shield equal to **100% of her Max HP** for 4s
- Shield is removed when Ciaccona is switched off the field
- This brief Shield activates Crown of Valor set bonuses at the moment of Liberation cast, providing a burst of ATK% and Crit. DMG% that aligns with her highest personal damage window

**Winds of Rinascita**
- Increases Heavy Attack — Quadruple Downbeat DMG by **30%**
- Effectively permanent passive — not conditional; simply makes Quadruple Downbeat, which is already her strongest single-hit cluster, 30% stronger at all times

## Ciaccona: Intro/Outro Skills

**Intro Skill — Roaming with the Wind**
- Deals **95.12% Aero DMG** and inflicts **1 stack of Aero Erosion** on the target
- Concerto Regen: 10
- Follow up with Basic Attack to immediately chain into **BA Stage 3** (skipping Stages 1 and 2 entirely)
- **+1 Musical Essence** on cast — means she enters the field with 1/3 stacks built before a single Basic Attack
- Critical rotation entry point: Intro → BA3 → BA4 → Jump → Mid-air → BA4 → Quadruple Downbeat is the condensed Sub-DPS rotation

**Outro Skill — Windcalling Tune**
- **Aero Erosion DMG dealt to targets near the active Resonator is Amplified by 100% for 30 seconds**
- One of the highest-value Outro effects in the game: doubles all Aero Erosion DoT damage for 30s, which in Cartethyia's kit (where Aero Erosion is a primary damage source and Wind's Indelible Imprint scales from stack count) represents an enormous raw multiplier
- 30s duration means it covers a full Main DPS rotation with margin to spare

## Ciaccona: Resonance Chains (Sequences)

**S1**
Casting Resonance Skill — Harmonic Allegro grants Ciaccona immunity to interruption for 3s. Casting Basic Attack increases Ciaccona's ATK by 35% for 10s.
*Value: Solid DPS upgrade — the ATK buff applies on any BA hit, so it's effectively permanent in her rotation. Interruption immunity on Harmonic Allegro provides QoL for the skill's repositioning function in content with aggressive enemy attacks.*

**S2**
During Resonance Liberation — Singer's Triple Cadenza, Resonators in the team gain 40% Aero DMG Bonus.
*Value: The team support breakpoint. In a Cartethyia team, this 40% Aero DMG Bonus activates during Recital (which persists off-field), meaning Cartethyia benefits from it throughout her entire field window. This is the single strongest team buff Ciaccona provides and the primary Sequence Node for competitive Aero Erosion teams. S0→S2 is the priority investment bracket.*

**S3**
Casting Basic Attack Stage 4 additionally grants +1 segment of Musical Essence. Resonance Skill — Harmonic Allegro gains 1 more charge.
*Value: Rotation speed and accessibility upgrade. Double BA4 Essence means reaching Quadruple Downbeat requires only 1 complete BA4 cycle instead of 2 (combined with the Intro Skill's inherent +1). Harmonic Allegro's extra charge provides more Aero Erosion application per rotation and more Concerto Regen cycles.*

**S4**
Ciaccona ignores 45% of the targets' DEF when dealing damage with Heavy Attack — Quadruple Downbeat. Ciaccona ignores 45% of the targets' DEF when dealing Resonance Liberation DMG.
*Value: Large personal damage increase on her two primary damage sources. The Liberation DEF ignore applies to all Tonic waves during Recital, making this a sustained off-field damage uplift throughout the full rotation.*

**S5**
Ciaccona gains 40% Resonance Liberation DMG Bonus. DMG taken by Resonators within and around the range of Resonance Liberation — Singer's Triple Cadenza is reduced by 30%.
*Value: Combined offense and team defense. The 30% DMG reduction within the Liberation radius adds genuine survivability value for the active Resonator during the critical burst window.*

**S6**
During Solo Concert, Ciaccona or Ensemble Sylph deals Aero DMG equal to 220% of Ciaccona's ATK to nearby targets, considered Resonance Liberation DMG.
*Value: Transforms the passive Solo Concert buff into an active damage source. Two Ensemble Sylphs with near-permanent uptime each dealing 220% ATK Aero DMG periodically becomes a substantial DPS contribution — and it benefits from S4's DEF ignore and S5's Resonance Liberation DMG Bonus. S6 Ciaccona functions as a Main DPS in AoE content.*

## Ciaccona: Recommended Echo Sets

**Best (Aero Erosion / Aero teams): Gusts of Welkin (5-pc)**
Gusts of Welkin: Inflicting Aero Erosion upon enemies increases Aero DMG for all Resonators in the team by 15%, and for the Resonator triggering the effect by an additional 15% (personal total: 30% Aero DMG Bonus, team total: 15% Aero DMG Bonus), lasting 20s. Since Ciaccona inflicts Aero Erosion on enemies through BA4, Skill, Intro, and Liberation, she maintains this buff continuously. Combined with her Ensemble Sylph Solo Concert (+24%) and her signature weapon's Aero DMG Bonus passive, the total Aero DMG buff she provides to the full team is extraordinary.

**Best (general / Spectro Frazzle teams): Moonlit Clouds (5-pc)**
Sacrifices Ciaccona's personal Aero DMG bonus for an ATK% buff to the incoming Resonator via Outro. Best when Ciaccona is used with non-Aero Main DPS (such as Phoebe in Spectro Frazzle teams). Weaker for Aero Erosion teams but provides broad synergy otherwise.

**Hybrid consideration:** Players who use Ciaccona in both Aero and non-Aero contexts may want to maintain two separate Echo sets — Prydwen explicitly flags this as a meaningful resource consideration.

**Main Echo:** Windcleaver (4-cost Aero)
The optimal main Echo for Ciaccona in Aero Erosion builds. Provides Aero DMG Bonus and contributes to Gusts of Welkin set count. Echo Skill deals multi-hit Aero DMG. Belongs to the Gusts of Welkin set.

**Alternative Main Echo:** Impermanence Heron (4-cost Energy Regen) — for Moonlit Clouds support builds; provides Energy Regen to sustain Liberation cycling while contributing to the Moonlit Clouds set.

**Echo Main Stats:**
- 4-cost: CRIT Rate (prioritized — her Forte node is CRIT DMG, making CRIT Rate the gap to fill)
- 3-cost #1: Aero DMG Bonus
- 3-cost #2: Aero DMG Bonus or ATK%
- 1-cost ×2: ATK%

**Sub-stat Priority:** CRIT Rate > CRIT DMG > ATK% > Energy Regen > Resonance Liberation DMG Bonus > Flat ATK
**Target Ratio:** 70–80% CRIT Rate / 250%+ CRIT DMG / ~120% Energy Regen

## Ciaccona: Best Weapon

**Signature — Woodland Aria (5★ Pistols)**
CRIT Rate sub-stat (the ideal complement to her CRIT DMG Forte node). Passive (R1): ATK +12%; inflicting Aero Erosion on the target grants 24% Aero DMG Bonus for 10s; hitting targets with Aero Erosion reduces their Aero RES by 10% for 20s. Since Ciaccona inflicts Aero Erosion continuously, the 24% Aero DMG Bonus is effectively permanent and applies to the entire team via Gusts of Welkin. The 10% Aero RES reduction is a team-wide damage amplifier, making Woodland Aria one of the highest-impact signature weapons in the game for team synergy. At R5: ATK +24%, Aero DMG Bonus +48%, Aero RES reduction 16%.

**Alternative — Cadenza (5★ Pistols)**
ATK sub-stat; grants Resonance Skill DMG Bonus significantly when Intro Skill or Liberation is cast (up to +48% for 5s extendable by Skill casts). Competitive with Woodland Aria for personal damage in Sub-DPS rotations; lacks the Aero RES reduction team buff.

**Alternative — Pistols of Night (5★ Pistols)**
CRIT DMG sub-stat; grants ATK and Resonance Liberation DMG Bonus on Liberation cast. Solid for Liberation-centric rotations; the CRIT DMG sub-stat is redundant with her Forte node but still effective at scale.

**F2P Option — Romance in Farewell (4★ Craftable Pistols)**
Craftable Rinascita weapon; increases ATK when the enemy is afflicted by a Negative Status effect. Since Ciaccona is an Aero Erosion/Spectro Frazzle enabler — enemies are almost always afflicted during her field time — this weapon's passive has near-perfect uptime. Notably strong F2P option rated competitive with lower-end 5-star alternatives at equivalent investment (Game8).

## Ciaccona: Best Teams

**S-Tier: Cartethyia + Ciaccona + Chisa**
- **Cartethyia** (Main DPS): Synergizes with Ciaccona's entire kit: Gusts of Welkin team Aero DMG Bonus, Woodland Aria Aero RES reduction, Outro +100% Aero Erosion DMG Amplification, Ensemble Sylph Solo Concert +24% Aero DMG Bonus, and Recital's continuous off-field Aero Erosion stacking into Cartethyia's Inherent Passive Wind's Indelible Imprint damage scaling
- **Ciaccona** (Sub-DPS/Enabler): Short rotation, low field time, high buff output; Recital persists through Cartethyia's full field window
- **Chisa** (Support/Healer): Increases the maximum Aero Erosion stack count by 3 (same function as Aero Rover but with healing, shielding, and DEF shred via Havoc Bane); consistent healing enables Cartethyia to safely reduce HP to 50% for Liberation
*Current benchmark team for Aero Erosion content. Prydwen rates Ciaccona T0 Hybrid in Tower of Adversity.*

**S-Tier: Cartethyia + Ciaccona + Aero Rover**
- **Aero Rover** (Sub-DPS/Support): Free unit with dedicated 5-star weapon; increases Aero Erosion stack limit by 3; Ciaccona's Inherent Passive (confirmed for Cartethyia's kit file) notes Rover: Aero restores 25 Windstrings on Omega Storm cast when Cartethyia is in team
- Best for players without Chisa; Aero Rover's damage contribution is meaningful even at low investment

**A-Tier: Phoebe + Ciaccona + Support**
- **Phoebe** (Main DPS / Spectro Frazzle): Ciaccona's yellow Tonic mode enables Spectro Frazzle application across multiple waves, which Phoebe previously lacked in Whimpering Wastes content — this pairing makes Phoebe tier-competitive in multi-wave formats she was previously underperforming in
- Requires switching Ciaccona's Liberation to yellow mode (hold/tap yellow button post-Liberation cast)
- One of the most frequently cited Ciaccona team pairings outside of Aero Erosion context

**A-Tier: Jiyan + Ciaccona + Aero Rover**
- **Jiyan** (Main DPS): Straightforward Aero DPS with excellent grouping and AoE; benefits from Gusts of Welkin Aero DMG Bonus and Ensemble Sylph Solo Concert
- Not as strong as Jiyan + Mortefi + Shorekeeper but viable as an alternative, particularly for players invested in Aero teams

## Ciaccona: DPS Benchmarks

Ciaccona is rated **T0 Hybrid in Tower of Adversity** and **T1 Hybrid in Whimpering Wastes** (Prydwen, Patch 3.0). Game8 rates her **SS-Tier** as Sub-DPS.

**Key performance notes:**
- In the Cartethyia + Ciaccona team, Ciaccona's contribution includes personal damage from Quadruple Downbeat + Liberation, the Gusts of Welkin team Aero DMG Bonus, the Outro +100% Aero Erosion Amplification, and continuous off-field Recital Tonic waves — making her DPS contribution in the team significantly higher than her personal damage number alone implies
- Her sequence value is intentionally low at S0: Prydwen notes this as both a pro (great value at S0) and a mild con (less incentive for high sequence investment compared to characters with dramatic S2 breakpoints)
- Her Recital continues across wave transitions in Whimpering Wastes, making her off-field Erosion/Frazzle application unusually consistent in multi-wave formats even at T1 performance
- As a Main DPS (extended BA4 chain rotation): her damage output is comparable to top-tier Sub-DPS units in AoE scenarios, particularly with S6's Solo Concert active damage

**Standard Sub-DPS Rotation Outline (Cartethyia team):**
1. Ciaccona enters via Intro Skill → 1 Aero Erosion, +1 Musical Essence, Concerto Regen 10; chain into BA Stage 3
2. BA Stage 3 → BA Stage 4 → Jump cancel → Ensemble Sylph generated; Mid-air 1 → Mid-air 2 → BA Stage 4 → Jump cancel (2nd Sylph; 2/3 Musical Essence) → Quadruple Downbeat (3/3 Essence consumed; 1 Aero Erosion, 25 Concerto Regen)
3. Harmonic Allegro → 1 Aero Erosion, 15 Concerto Regen
4. Resonance Liberation → Improvised Symphonic Poem (553.50% hit; Recital begins; 2 or more Tonic waves before swap)
5. Switch to yellow or green Tonic mode as appropriate → Swap to Cartethyia; Ciaccona continues Recital auto-Tonics off-field
6. Outro Skill active on swap: +100% Aero Erosion DMG Amplification for 30s
